# More ways to build, and a better look
Date: 2026-11-13
Kind: news

This note lists what changed since 11 November: more ways to play every class, a limit of three keystones and a round of
animation fixes.

The passive tree has no new version, so old build codes and saved trees stay valid. The keystone limit below is the one
exception.

Found a bug? Use System > Report a Bug in the game, or tell us on Discord. Every report is read.

## New

- **Three ways to play every class.** Each class has three Recommended builds in the Auto build list. Each one has its
  own keystones and a line that names its price.
- **Starter Builds guide.** The website has a Starter Builds page with the three Recommended builds of every class. It
  says in words what each one does, how it plays, what it costs, which keystones it walks to, which spells to lean on
  and what to wear. Each build has import codes for levels 100, 300, 500 and 1000. Paste one in the Build Code panel of
  the Passive Tree.
- **Save to a loadout slot.** The Auto build panel has a Save to a loadout slot button. Pick a slot and the build goes
  into it, named after the build. Your live tree does not change. The gear, runes and Paragon of that slot stay as they
  were. A slot that already holds a build asks you first. The active slot and the locked slots cannot be picked. It
  needs level 15.
- **Auto rotation variants.** Next to the default list, the Auto rotation now has One target and Groups. The Thief,
  Ninja, Samurai, Ranger, Deadeye and Beastmaster also have Mines. The tooltip of each shows what it gains and what it
  gives up. The default list is unchanged. A level with no variant uses the default.
- **Class sections for the Warrior, Ranger and Mystic lines.** These three lines get what the other four have: line
  nodes, a path for each ascension with two notables and a keystone, and a mastery. Nothing that was in the tree moved.
  - Six new keystones, one for each ascension: Unbroken Line for the Juggernaut, Open Veins for the Berserker, Lone Mark
    for the Deadeye, Wolfbond for the Beastmaster, Convergence for the Elementalist and Phantom Double for the
    Illusionist. Each has a price, shown in its tooltip.
- **Three more keystones.** Each has a price.
  - Momentum, for the Ranger, Thief and Monk lines: your hits deal 1% more for every 5% of movement speed above your
    base, up to 10% more. You take 8% more damage.
  - Attunement, for the Mystic, Necromancer and Templar lines: the element your tree favours most deals 11% more. Your
    other elements deal 15% less.
  - Open Wounds, for the Monk and Templar lines: melee hits with bare fists or a one-handed weapon make the target bleed
    for 40% of the hit over 4 s. Those hits deal 8% less.
- **Spell modifier masteries.** The mastery nodes of the seven class lines get 20 new effects in all, two or three a
  line. A new effect gives one named spell a free support effect: Fork, Echo, Breadth, Shatter, Hex, Quickening or a
  brand. It is a Common support rune at 75% of its strength, and it takes no rune socket. A real rune of the same
  support on that spell counts when it is stronger. The two never add. You still hold one effect per mastery. The effect
  works once you have learned its spell. The Spell Supports window does not list it.
- **Weapon families.** Sword, axe and club now play differently. This is for the basic melee attack of the Warrior,
  Thief and Templar lines. The Monk line, fists, bows, wands and spells are not changed. Every sword, axe and club
  tooltip has one gold line that says what it does.
  - Sword, tempo. Each hit on the same target speeds up your swing, up to 20% faster. Each hit is a little lighter, so
    your damage per second stays about the same, with up to 2% more at full speed. A new target starts over.
  - Axe, cleave. 14% of each hit is split among the monsters next to the target. It costs 2.5% damage on one target.
  - Club, impact. A hit has a 25% chance to daze a monster. It cannot attack for 0.7 s, then it rests for 3 s. A boss is
    only slowed. It costs 2.5% damage on one target.
  - Against players there is no cleave and no daze, and the sword gets half the speed.
- **Card sets.** Turn on 3 or more cards that share one element, one for each spell you cast, and you get a set bonus.
  Only one set works at a time. Fire adds 4% fire damage. Earth poisons the target for 4% of the hit over 4 s. Death
  heals you for 1.5% of the damage, at most 1% of your health a hit. Holy heals you for 0.5% of your health, at most
  once every 3 s. Energy slows the target by 15% for 1.5 s. Ice and Drown have no set. Against players the damage and
  poison bonuses are a third and there is no slow. Character Info shows your set and how close you are to one.
- **Thirteen new unique items.** The Mystic, Necromancer and Thief lines get 13 unique items for levels 430 to 880.
  Each one carries a passive made for one way to build. Some examples: spells that echo onto a second monster, stronger
  minions that cost you spell damage, a kill that sets off a burst of fire. They drop from monsters, like the other
  uniques.
- **Uniques explain themselves.** The tooltip of a unique says under its power whether it works for your class. On a
  unique you wear it also says whether the power is active or asleep, and why. Right-click a unique and choose Open in
  Codex to open its page. The Codex has a new Basics page, Uniques. The Uniques tab filters by class, its Level filter
  reaches the highest unique, and the page of a leveling unique lists the monster levels it also drops from. The Item
  Finder finds a unique by its own name. The first unique you find tells you what a unique is. No unique changed.
- **Support role in the Party Finder.** The Party Finder has a fourth role, Support, in violet with the letter S. The
  Illusionist, Iron Sage, Necromancer, Lich, Bone Lord, Templar, Inquisitor and Sentinel can take it, and keep their
  other roles. A group never has to ask for one. In a battleground a support counts as damage.
- **Death and respawn effects.** A monster that dies shows a short effect that fits its kind: blood, undead, fire,
  energy or venom. A boss shows a large one. A monster that comes back on the map shows a small puff.
- **Glows on the ground.** A soft glow shows under the Ember portal, the health and mana globes and the loot sack. At
  most 24 glow at once, the nearest first.
- **Spell effects that fit.** 46 spells now show an effect that fits them. Holy spells no longer use the blue arcane
  blast. Wind spells no longer use the red critical-hit star. The Monk finishers no longer use the fire comet. Quick
  hits no longer hold one picture for two seconds. Damage, area and timing did not change.
- **Potion upgrades explained.** A new How it works card, Potion upgrades, says what each Everfull tier needs and gives, where to upgrade and what it costs. Search for potion in Help Search (Alt+F). When your level reaches a tier you can take, one message tells you once and says where to go.

## Changes

- **At most 3 keystones.** A character holds at most 3 keystones. Every keystone of the tree counts, masteries do not.
  The tree shows Keystones 2/3, and a keystone you cannot take says why. A build code, an Auto build or a loadout with
  more is refused whole, never trimmed. A loadout is checked when you load it. A tree that holds more than 3 when you
  log in has all its points refunded for free, and a message says why. Auto builds stop at 3.
- **Loadouts: 5 free slots, 8 at most.** You start with 5 loadout slots, was 3. A 6th opens at level 200 and a 7th at
  level 500. The 8th comes with the Chaos Pact.
- **Five keystones tuned.** The tooltips show the new numbers.
  - Aether Edge without Hollow Vessel adds 33% of your current energy shield as energy damage, was 6%.
  - Hollow Vessel without Aether Edge gives back 110% of the health you give up as energy shield, was 50%. The health
    percent from your tree now counts in it.
  - A character that holds Aether Edge and Hollow Vessel together is unchanged.
  - Open Hand Creed adds 36% damage, was 10%. The Knuckles of the Unbound without the keystone are unchanged. The strike
    spells of the Thief line keep their weapon attack.
  - Winter's Lattice shatters deal 5% of the maximum health, was 6%.
  - Sigil of Cinders pulses every second even when you keep casting. Before, each cast on the bearer started the clock
    over, so only one cast in three pulsed.
- **Mines for the Ranger line.** The Ranger, Deadeye and Beastmaster can cast Seeker Mine and Detonate Mines from level
  60, as the Thief line does. The Ranger gave up Radiant Shard and the Deadeye gave up Skewering Line, so each still has
  13 attack spells. The Beastmaster keeps every spell and holds the mines on top, with 14.
- **Weather.** The dust and ember specks are bigger and brighter. Rain is the same.
- **Spell damage reads every input.** A spell's damage now follows your level, your magic level and, for a class that
  fights with a weapon, your weapon skill and the weapon's attack. The Warrior, Ranger, Thief and Monk lines gain a
  third of a percent for every magic level above the usual one for their class, and lose the same below it. A character
  with the usual magic level deals what it dealt before. Four spells that ignored an input read it now: Lay on Hands,
  the Iron Sage's Kindled Breath, Kindling Spark and Bewitching Hex. The Spell damage card in How it works says what a
  spell is made of.
- **Companions, snares and ticks take your bonuses.** The Beastmaster's companion bite and the pet's Spark Aura count
  as your damage now: the passive tree, gems, charms, item modifiers and the other bonuses reach them, and so does the
  armour of the monster. The bite of a character with no tree and no gear is the same as before. The poison of
  Blightswarm, the burns of Witchfire Bolt and Falling Pyre and the curse of Shattered Psyche tick as spell hits: they
  can crit and they take support runes, cards and the other spell bonuses. Each tick keeps its size. Support runes and
  spell modifier masteries work in the hits of Snare Trap and Seeker Mine. Rift Cleaver, Mirror Shards, Rending Pounce,
  Grave Nova, Gutting Slash and Gale Volley have support sockets.
- **Blessed Earth follows you.** The holy zone of the Ranger, Deadeye and Beastmaster now moves with you. Each of its 6 pulses hits enemies and heals allies around where you stand at that moment. Damage, healing, mana and cooldown are the same.
- **One pool for increased damage.** The damage bonuses used to multiply each other. Now they add up in one pool, and the pool counts once. It holds the passive tree and the Spell Damage of a wand or rod, Paragon, unique powers, gems, a damage mastery, a card set, charms and the sword's tempo edge. Up to +150 the pool counts in full, above it each point counts less, as before. Strength and intelligence, the weapon and its Weapon Damage, critical hits, support runes and class damage still multiply on their own. New Codex page: Basics, Increased damage. The Character Info and the tree window show the pool of each damage type. **This makes strong stacks weaker.** The more sources a character stacked, the more it loses; a character that holds few bonuses loses little or gains a little.
  - Unique powers. The damage bonus of a power counts at most +10 for one power and +40 for all together. Before, up to five powers multiplied to 1.8 times the damage. A power's price (the damage it takes away) is unchanged, so some powers are now worth little: read the line on the item. The critical hit powers, the conversion of Winter Bite and its kind, and the minion pact are not changed.
  - Gems. A damage gem counts at most 25 per element, was 40. Physical gems stay at 12.
  - Damage masteries. The four brand masteries (Hallowed Ground, Storm Rupture, Grave Wail and Bell of Dawn) no longer give a free brand. They give +3 increased damage while their spell is cast. The other 16 effects are unchanged.
  - Card sets. The Fire set gives +4 increased damage, at most 4 for a card set.
  - Charms and weapon families. Increased damage to a monster family and the sword's tempo edge count at most 6 together. Charms keep their numbers, what they hold above 6 counts for nothing. Their tooltip now says Increased damage to ...
  - Paragon. Damage points add to the pool, they no longer multiply it.
- **Duels between players.** A hit that a player deals to another player is now scaled by the attacker's level, so duels last as long as they did: x1.00 at level 100, x0.94 at 200, x0.89 at 300, x0.79 at 500, x0.76 at 700, x0.48 at 1000, x0.52 at 1500 and x0.46 at 2000, with a straight line in between. Summons and companions use their owner's level. Monsters and healing are never scaled.
- **Gear keeps growing.** The item level of a drop now follows a flatter, longer curve. It keeps rising to monster level
  2200, where it was nearly flat after 1100. Drops are a little lower up to about monster level 1600 and higher above it:
  item level 62 at monster level 300 (was 67), 117 at 1000 (was 123), 144 at 2000 (was 138). The top tier, T8, drops from a
  boss of monster level about 1060 (was 940) and from a Rare of about 1230. Items you own keep their tier and their numbers.
- **Better tiers roll more often.** Among the tiers an item level allows, each tier is 1.35 times as likely as the one
  below it, so T8 is about 8 times as likely as T1 (before, every tier was as likely as any other). The value ranges of
  the tiers did not change. The orbs follow the same odds, and the Codex shows them.
- **A ceiling on critical hits.** A character counts at most 45 points of critical chance and 150% extra damage on a
  critical hit, all sources together: gear, gems, the passive tree, Paragon, buffs and Keen Edge. What is above that is
  lost, so a build that stacked critical hits loses about 30% of its damage. Character Info says "cap" when your total was
  cut, and the item tooltip shows the cap. Critical Strike Chance and Critical Strike Damage now roll on weapons, necklaces
  and rings only. A badge or a quiver that already has one keeps it, and it counts under the cap. Nothing was rerolled.
- **Mythic damage runes count as Legendary.** A Mythic Brand, Echo, Hex or Keen Edge rune keeps its name and its look and
  counts at its Legendary value: brand 50% (was 60), Echo 30% (was 35), Hex 6% (was 8), Keen Edge 6% (was 8). The tooltip
  of the rune says so. The other runes did not change.
- **A ceiling on penetration.** A character counts at most 20 points of physical and 20 points of elemental penetration,
  all sources together. The Sunder rune adds to the elemental 20 after your gear: with 12 points on your gear it gives
  at most 8.
- **No jump in monster health at level 650.** Monsters of the hunts from level 600 to 900 have less health: about half at
  650 to 700, 30% less at 800 and 15% less at 900. The jump in toughness at 650 is gone, and health now rises evenly all
  the way to level 1000. Levels 1000 and up are as before. You kill them faster, so each kill gives less
  experience, gold and loot. Experience, gold and rare gear per hour are the same, and a kill-count goal on these
  monsters asks for more kills, so it takes as long as before.

## Fixes

- The Death Mage no longer vanishes when it turns sideways.
- Attack speed from your tree and your gear no longer adds on top of itself when your gear updates. It used to climb to
  the cap.
