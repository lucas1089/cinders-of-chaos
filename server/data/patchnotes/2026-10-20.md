# Fairer fights, one place to hunt
Date: 2026-10-20

A big balance and quality pass. Area damage is now the same all over its area, the hunt hall is sorted by level, five
service NPCs take over menu chores, the passive tree can build itself, and the junk is gone from the loot. Numbers in
the balance section are calculated, not measured in game yet.

## New

- **Service NPCs.** Tavish Coldhand (stash), Vessa Hollowmark (pocket boss), Cassian Auric (bank), Halvard Deedkeeper
  (house broker) and Pell Ashmere (services) do what the matching menu windows do. Walk up and say hi.
- **Auto build.** The passive tree window has an Auto build button: pick a focus theme and it spends your points along
  a path for it. Check the result before you accept it.
- **Primary and secondary stats.** Each node now shows which stats matter most for your class, and the tree has a short
  stat help.
- **Class lanes.** Every class has marked lanes in the tree for its main play styles.
- **Seven new ascension spells.** Bastion Cry and Anvil Fall (Juggernaut), Twin Verdict (Deadeye), Rampage Leap
  (Berserker), Glacial Doom (Elementalist), Pack Frenzy (Beastmaster) and Shuriken Tempest (Ninja). Eight ascensions
  still have none; they come later.
- **Damage number scatter.** Damage numbers spread out a little so they stop piling on top of each other. Three
  strengths: Subtle, Normal (default) and Wide. Saved per character.
- **HP and mana percent.** Right click the health or mana bar to show the percent. Saved per character.
- **Error messages in colour.** Game errors show in a clear red-orange so you notice them.
- **Hunt hall by level.** The quest hall now has its own room with all 16 quests. The hunts are on three floors by
  level: 1-500, 500-1000 and 1000+. Ten new hunts for low levels. Each portal in the hub says what level it needs.

## Changes

- **Cleanse spells are gone.** The spells that cleared status effects are retired for every class. Their slots were
  reused for the new ascension spells above.
- **Area damage is uniform.** Area spells (Chaos spells and stock ones alike) hit every target in the area for the same
  damage, with no falloff from the centre.
- **Absorb ceiling at 75%.** Absorb from gear, rarity and the passive tree together is capped at 75% per element.
- **Life and mana leech.** Leech now counts the damage that really landed, not the damage the hit could have done.
- **Fire-immune monsters** now take 70% of fire damage instead of none. Your pets stay immune.
- **Shadow Mend** heals a little less and can be used every 1.5 seconds. Its heal comes from level, magic level and 5% of max health.
- **Stackables never roll rarity.** Runes, stars and other stackable items lose their rarity and keep stacking.
- **Throwing weapons** (spears, stars, snowballs and similar) are removed from the game, except the snowball. The starter
  kits of Thief, Ninja and Samurai no longer carry throwing stars.
- **Loot.** 52 junk items no longer drop. The creature products tab in the stash is not fed any more.
- **Drown nodes** are hidden in the passive tree. Points spent there come back. Ascension nodes no longer have a cost or
  a downside; the tree was refunded once at login. Avatar of Iron now belongs to the Berserker.
- **Annihilator** can be done alone.
- **Corpses** fade faster.

## Fixes

- **Stash list.** With many items the stash list could come back empty. It now sends smaller pages.
- **Runes in closed backpacks.** The helper and hotkeys can use runes that sit in a closed backpack.
- **Use with the cursor.** After using an item with the crosshair, the cursor stays on the item and works over window
  borders.
- **Spellbook.** The spell picker shows the spells of your own class, the same list as the helper.
- **Minimap after death.** The minimap overlay opens again by itself after you respawn.
- **Leech** no longer breaks on odd values: the amount comes from the health actually lost.
- **Hub portals** check your level and tell you the level you need.
- **Muted players.** Staff can silence a player's chat and shadow mute them.

## Balance

- **Area spells recalculated.** The rotation tables were redone for uniform area damage. Ranger and Beastmaster mana
  runway at level 60 was checked again and fixed.
- **New spells.** Their numbers come from the model, not from play tests. Tell us what feels off.
