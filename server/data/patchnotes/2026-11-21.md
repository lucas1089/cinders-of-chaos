# Spell upgrades
Date: 2026-11-21
Kind: news

Your spells grow now. Every damage and heal spell has a rank, a choice of Forms and combos with other spells, and in the
endgame the best of them awaken. Monsters grow tougher along the game to match.

Everyone starts at rank zero, old characters too. Nothing you did before counts as a rank, and nothing was taken from you.

Found a bug? Use System > Report a Bug in the game, or tell us on Discord. Every report is read.

## New

- **Spell ranks.** Each damage and heal spell has a rank from 0 to 20. A rank grows with Spell Points. You earn them from
  the experience of monsters you kill: the kill is shared between your spells by the damage each one dealt to that
  monster, so the spell you really use grows. Nothing else gives Spell Points. A rank adds damage or healing, a shorter
  cooldown and a lower mana cost. Rank 20 is about 18% more damage or healing, and the shorter cooldown comes on top. Open the Spellbook and its Upgrades tab to see
  the rank, the points to go and what the next rank gives.
- **Forms.** At rank 10 and again at rank 20 you pick one of two Forms of a spell. A Form changes how the spell plays: a
  split, a chain, a ground effect, a pull, a delayed burst or another element. The first pick of each tier is free. A
  change costs gold, and a change of the last pick within 30 minutes is free, for a misclick.
- **Combos.** Cast two spells within four seconds and the second one hits 10% to 20% harder. Every class has the element
  reactions, for example ice then fire. Each class also has combos of its own that open at rank 5 on both spells. A label
  over the target says when a combo lands. Three new mastery effects bend the timing.
- **Awakening.** At rank 20 with both Forms picked, a spell can be awakened: a new name, a new look and a signature
  effect, and 10% more on every hit. You can awaken up to three spells. It costs Seals: Waystone Seals from tier XVI to XXV
  clears, Rift Seals from Greater Rift runs that end at tier 16 or higher, and Pinnacle Seals from the Ascended bosses.
  Seals are counters, not items, so they cannot be traded or stolen.

## Changes

- **Monsters have more health as you climb.** From level 100 monsters have a little more health, about 2% at level 100,
  22% at level 1000 and 39% at level 2500. A character with upgrades kills at the pace it did before. A character with
  no upgrades is slower and earns less experience an hour.
- **PvP is gentler on upgrades.** Against other players, ranks and Forms count at half. Combos and the awakening effects
  do not work in PvP.
- **Omen guardian Morvane the Hollow Crown has more health.** Alone you killed it faster than the other two Omen guardians.
  It now takes about as long as they do, five minutes alone and under five with a party of five.
