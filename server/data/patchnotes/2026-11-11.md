# Smoother walking and a tidier hotbar
Date: 2026-11-11
Kind: news

Thank you for beta weekend 2. This note lists what changed since 7 November: walking that flows, and a round of fixes
for the new inventory panel, hotbar and floating chat.

Found a bug? Use System > Report a Bug in the game, or tell us on Discord. Every report is read.

## New

- **Walking that flows and eight directions.** Steps now run into each other instead of stopping and starting, whether
  you hold a key, click to walk or watch monsters move. Diagonal movement (Smart Walk) is on by default, once per
  computer. Press W and D, or Up and Right, together to walk the diagonal at once. The numpad keys 1, 3, 7 and 9 walk
  the diagonals with NumLock on or off. A numpad digit set as a hotkey no longer fires while NumLock is on.
- **Purse button.** The new inventory panel has a Purse button that opens your purse.

## Changes

- **Hotbar keys.** The hotbar slots use the keys 1 to 8, and Q and E for the two side sockets. Keys you set yourself
  are kept.
- **Orb size** in the options now also sizes the health and mana globes docked in the hotbar.
- **Stack counts** in the inventory panel are bright and easier to read.
- **Monster bar.** The level plate left of the monster's name is gone.
- **Layout options.** There is a Layout entry in the options for the hotbar and the floating chat, and the chat header
  and its left limit are fixed.
- **Next server update.** This needs the new server program and arrives with it: monsters leave a sack of loot instead of
  their body. The sack holds 60 slots and lasts 3 minutes. Player bodies stay as they are.

## Fixes

- The hotbar and the floating chat stay as you set them after a death and after any relog.
- The E socket of the hotbar takes dropped items again.
- A reflected hit, such as the Reflective monster modifier or a rift's Reflection, is no longer counted twice. One hit
  could kill a player.
