# Awakened spell looks
Date: 2026-11-22
Kind: news

Awakened spells have their own look now. With the new game client, an awakened spell plays a new effect of its element
when you cast it, with a sigil ring that turns while it plays, and its projectile is new too.

Found a bug? Use System > Report a Bug in the game, or tell us on Discord. Every report is read.

## New

- **A look for every awakened spell.** The effect follows the element of the spell and its shape: a single target, a
  circle, a ring around you, a line or a wave. A Form that changes the element of a spell changes the look with it.
  Spells without an element of their own take the arcane look. Damage, area and timing do not change.
- **Needs the new client.** Update the game client to see it. An older client keeps the usual effects.
