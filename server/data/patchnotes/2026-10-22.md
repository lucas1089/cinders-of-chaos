# Wings, crowns and capes
Date: 2026-10-22

A cosmetics update and a haunted event. The store gets 89 new wings and two new categories, Head Accessories and Capes. Six of the new
pieces are never sold: only bosses drop them. Nothing here changes your power.

## New

- **The Haunted Harvest.** A cursed harvest village opens for levels 450-650, from October 25 to November 5 each year:
  pumpkins to harvest, a corn maze, a graveyard whose dead climb out, a crypt under the ruined chapel and the Hollow
  King at its heart. A Harvest Portal next to the temples of Cinderhold and Mirewake takes you there. Hollow candy buys
  potions, orbs and haunted cosmetics from Widow Hessa.
- **Infinity Tower rebalanced.** Floors now follow the level they are meant for: fewer monsters (4 to 9), softer
  tiers and monsters that hit like a normal hunt. Zeburoth and Ashvoran moved to floors 110 and 120, so a level 800
  character gets about 38 floors deep instead of stopping at 29. Every 5th floor pays a first-clear reward once per
  season (Crystal Coins, Orbs of Upheaval and Fate) and a smaller one on every clear. Monsters drop tower orbs and
  coins. The window and the entry message show the next reward.
- **23 new tales.** Archivist Maelis, next to the temple of Cinderhold, now has 27 story quests, from level 8 to
  2000. Twelve are below level 200; the first waits for you at level 8. Some tales send you to a place, which shows on
  your map, or to a faction leader to ask about "tales". Five of them are chains: finish one to open the next. They pay
  experience, gold to your bank, orbs, faction reputation and six new lore books.
- **89 new wings.** Animated wings of fire, frost, storm, earth, light and shadow, from bat and raven wings to
  stained glass and seraph wings, in the store's Wings category for 250 or 300 Chaos Coins. They sit centred on your
  back in every direction.
- **Head Accessories.** A new store category of 90 animated horns, ears, halos, crowns, antlers and orbiting charms,
  150 or 200 Chaos Coins. A head accessory takes the aura slot: you wear one aura, head accessory or cape at a time.
  Choose it in the Outfit window like an aura.
- **Capes.** A new store category of 88 animated capes, cloaks, hoods and mantles, 200 or 250 Chaos Coins. A cape also
  takes the aura slot.
- **Six boss exclusives.** Void Raven Wings (Nyxareth the Void Archon), Demon Rend Wings (Kaelgor the Infernal
  Warlord), Frost Crystal Wings (Skaldra the Rime Queen), the Eclipse Halo (the Ascended Omens), the Spider Crown (the
  Ashen Sepulchre's wardens) and the Vampire Collar Cape (Vharok the Blood Colossus): a rare personal drop, never sold.
  They show in the Collection tab of the Cosmetics window.
- **51 new monsters in the late hunts.** Hexwood Thicket, Corsairs' Reach, Mirefang Basin, Rimecrown Depths,
  Slagmourn Foundry, Cinderglass Sanctum, Hollowmass Reliquary, Gallowfen Mire, Voidscar Chasm, Gravehorn Deeps and
  Duskmarrow Crater get new creatures: Gallows Oaks, Ironmask raiders, Crypt Crabs, Wax Penitents, Pyre Storks and
  many more. Each hunts like a monster it shares the area with, so the hunts are as hard and pay as well as before;
  only the faces are new.
- **41 new hunts fill the teleport hall's upper floors.** Every empty room of floors 6 and 5 now leads to a hunt of
  its own: 31 for levels 500-1050 on floor 6, 10 for levels 1050-2000 on floor 5, from Gloomtooth Caverns to the
  Worldscar Abyss. Each has a safe camp with a portal back to the hall, and most have a reward chest to find.
- **Old looks back.** Lacewing Moths, Tundra Trolls, Loricate Orgers, Ironhook Corsairs, Mercenaries, Swamp Snappers,
  Webbound Crawlers, Gargoyles, Werewolves, Goblins, War Wolves, Orc Riders, Carrion Worms, Skeleton Warriors, Ghosts
  and Witches keep the look you know. The new hunt monsters borrow the look of a cousin for now.
- **New spell effects.** Over a hundred spell and monster effects and 35 projectiles are redrawn, and 42 spells get
  effects of their own, from Dawnpiercer's holy strike to Blightswarm's wasps and Iaijutsu's steel crescent.
  Critical hits, dodges and reflects have new animations. Only the look changed: damage, areas and timing are the same.
- **A new level-up effect.** A ring of light, a rising star and the words LEVEL UP when you or a player near you gains a level. The
  Options switches for the level-up wings now turn this effect on and off.
- **New loot beams.** Loot your filter highlights now stands under an animated beam of light: silver for common,
  blue for magic and rare, fire for legendary, rainbow for divine, green for uniques and ancient for orbs.
- Update the client to see the new effects.
- **Moon outfits.** Four new outfits in the store's Outfits category, 750 Chaos Coins each with both addons: Starweaver,
  Crescent Oracle, Moonblade Mystic and Moonflame Mystic. White-robed seers with a violet crescent, for men and women,
  in your own colours, on foot or mounted.
- **Costumes.** A new store category of six fun costumes, 300 Chaos Coins each: two plush bears, three crawling hands
  and a walking box of fries. They have no colours or addons. Update the client to see them.
- **Previews.** The store and the Outfit window show the new pieces on your character before you buy or wear them.
- **The classic Magic Shield is back for Mystics, Necromancers and Rangers.** Ward of the Mind returns to the Mystic,
  Elementalist, Illusionist, Necromancer, Lich, Bone Lord, Ranger, Deadeye and Beastmaster from level 14 (50 mana, 200 seconds). While it is up, every hit takes
  your mana first and only what your mana cannot cover reaches your health. It does not count toward the 15-spell
  limit. No other class can learn it; Soulward stays everyone's short mana shield. Tiered monsters, bosses, raids,
  pocket bosses, invasions and the Vocation Trials now hit your mana as hard as they would hit your health.
- **Changelog.** A Changelog tab in the Patch Notes window lists every server and client change, newest first, with
  the date it went live and the client update that brought it. Filter it by Server, Client, Content or Website; changes
  since your last look are marked NEW. The top line shows when the server was last updated and which client update you
  run.
- **Leveling guide.** The website wiki has a new step by step guide from level 1 to 1000: where to hunt in each
  bracket, which systems open and how to reach them. Wiki > Guides > Leveling guide, in English and Portuguese.
- **Minion spells.** Every Necromancer, Lich and Bone Lord minion now casts two spells: one on a single target and one
  on an area. They use the area spell when two or more enemies are close. The summoned Risen Warrior also taunts nearby
  monsters, like the Bone Golem.
- **World Hunt.** A new world buff in the store (World Buffs, 300 coins). For one hour magic and rare monsters appear 50%
  more often, everywhere and for everyone. Buying again adds an hour, up to three.
- **Grave Nova.** A new Necromancer spell at level 50, `drai nyx oss`: death damage on every enemy within 3 sqm of
  you, no target needed. 80 mana, 6 s cooldown. Cursed monsters take the curse's extra damage from it too.
- **Waystones from level 1100 to 2500.** There are 15 Waystone Tiers now. Tier I opens a map of level 1100 monsters,
  and each tier adds 100 levels, up to level 2500 in Tier XV. Tiers VII to XV are new. Tiers I-V open one of four
  low maps, VI-X one of four middle maps, XI-XV one of four high maps. The device window shows each tier's level.
  Waystones dropped inside a map are of its tier or up to two above it, and its boss always drops the next tier.
  Outside the maps you find Tier I.
- **Magic and Rare waystones.** A waystone can now carry modifiers, like a Rift Key. A Normal waystone has none, a
  Magic one has up to one prefix and one suffix, a Rare one up to three of each. Prefixes make the map's monsters
  stronger: more health, damage or speed, elemental brands, reflection, breaches, a stronger boss. Suffixes make the map
  harder for you: no regeneration, less healing, elemental hexes, burning ground. Every modifier adds item quantity, item
  rarity, experience and a chance of more waystones in that map. Waystones that drop inside a map can come Magic or
  Rare. Use the waystone you want on the device: its portals take that one.
- **Orbs on waystones.** Awakening makes a Normal waystone Magic, Gilding makes it Rare, Grafting adds a modifier, Fate
  turns a Magic one Rare, Upheaval rerolls, Unmaking takes one off and Erasure makes it Normal again.
- **Waystone Atlas.** Adventure > Waystone Atlas. Kill a map's boss in a waystone run to complete that map at that
  tier, and the Rare cell of the tier when the waystone was Rare: each one gives a point, 75 in all. Spend them on a map
  of 81 nodes: more item quantity, rarity and experience, more waystones, higher tiers, stronger modifier rewards. The
  Low, Middle and High Maps branches count only in their maps. Four keystones, one at a time. The atlas of the player
  who opens the map counts for everyone inside.
- **Leave Boss Room.** Info & Services > Services has a Leave Boss Room button. Inside a boss room it takes you out
  to the room's exit, but not while you are in a fight. If you were the last one inside, the room closes and frees up.

## Changes

- **Real numbers again.** Damage, healing, health, the DPS meter, the combat log, the death recap, the damage boards
  and item tooltips no longer grow with your level. Every level now sees the real numbers of the game, so a hit of
  2,600 shows as 2,600, not 2.6M. Nothing got stronger or weaker: only the shown numbers changed.
- **Rings and necklaces are real equipment.** They no longer change when you put them on, run out of time or lose
  charges. Each one shows its stats and keeps them while worn. Like weapons and armour they drop with a rarity, an
  item level, prefixes and suffixes, and you can craft, socket, trade and auto loot them. Monsters and bounty bosses
  now drop them, from the life ring at the start to the butterfly ring and cobra amulet late. The stealth, energy,
  time, dwarven and might rings, the amulet of loss, the stone skin amulet and the four elemental 5-charge amulets
  stay as they were and never get a rarity. Rings and amulets you already have lose their timer and charges the
  next time you move them or log in.
- **New ring and necklace icons.** The 29 rings and necklaces that drop have their own icons. Update the client to
  see them.
- **Ring of the Last Ember no longer runs out.** Heart of Cinders and Heartbleed Pendant keep their charges too. If
  you wear an old copy, it goes to your backpack when you log in; put it on again.
- **The badge slot replaces the second ring slot.** Where the ammunition slot was, you now wear a badge. Bosses drop
  badges and amplifier catalysts again, and a catalyst upgrades your badge one tier. You wear one ring again; a ring
  you wore in the old second slot is in your Purse.
- **Decks, ships, belts and gloves can't be worn.** Carry a deck and use a card inside it to turn the card on or
  off. Carry a ship and walk from a dock onto the sea to sail. Belts and gloves have no use, so the armorsmith no
  longer crafts them; the Loot Buyer buys the ones you have.
  The boss roulette no longer offers decks or ships, so its other prizes come up more often.
- **Bounties give real experience.** A contract pays 40% of the experience of its kills; bosses pay at least a hunt.
- **Kill tasks give real experience.** A Bestiary kill task pays 20% of the experience of its kills.
- **Late-game experience back on track.** Level-scaled monsters and generated areas paid several times too much experience per kill. They now give what the leveling plan intends.
- **Late-game kill counts fit again.** Bounties, kill tasks and the Bestiary need less than half the kills of level-scaled monsters. A contract takes about as long as planned.
- **Passive tree attributes cap later.** Strength, intelligence, dexterity, vitality and spirit filled their caps
  from the nodes on your way to other nodes, so later attribute nodes did nothing. The old cap now gives 80% of the
  old maximum, and the points after it give the last 20% up to a new cap: strength and intelligence 60 points for
  +21% damage and 120 for +26%, dexterity 20 points for +19% weapon damage and 80 for +25%, vitality and spirit 50
  points for +40% health or mana and 100 for +50%. The most you can get is the same as before, and every point counts
  until the new cap. Vocation Trial and Paragon health and mana are not affected. Your tree stays as it is.
- **Thief and Ninja resistances.** Their trees gave far more dexterity than anyone can use. Five Attribute nodes of
  the Thief and three of the Ninja now give +2% resistance to every element instead of 10 dexterity.
- **Boss trophies craft into stones.** At the crafting table, General Crafting turns two boss trophies into a Myst,
  Seraphic or Dawnforged stone, and a stone plus a Runic Stone Shard into its emblem. Each recipe costs 25,000 gold.
- **Tooltips say what an item is used in.** A dim "Used in" line names up to two recipes and counts the rest.
  Update your client to see it.
- **Smoldering Aura is a small bonus now.** It burned a whole pack for about as much as your own attacks, often
  more, even while you stood still. Now it burns only while you fight (you attacked a monster in the last 5
  seconds), never from a protection zone and never through walls. A full pack takes about a tenth of your own
  damage from it, the same share at every level, and its tiers are much lower. An aura you already have keeps its
  tier and counts as the best roll of that tier today.
- **Item powers are bonuses, not a second build.** The damage of every item power no longer gets your passive tree,
  class bonuses, keystones, Execute or on-hit effects. Death Blast, Glacial Ward and Ember Step hit less hard, so
  each adds about a tenth of your damage in a fight, not up to all of it. Shatter Splash now bursts for a share of
  the health the killing blow took, split among the monsters it hits; its tiers are lower, and old items count as
  the best roll of their tier. Echo Shot, Critical Echo and Arc Lightning no longer get your tree bonus twice.
- **Cleaner monster art.** Rift monsters, omen guardians, raid bosses, faction mounts and other monsters now use
  fewer, cleaner colours with less pixel noise. Shapes, glowing eyes and outlines stay as they were. Update the client
  to see it.
- **Smarter Auto Target.** The helper's Auto Target now only picks monsters it can reach: a walkable way for melee, a
  clear line of sight for the ranged classes, so it no longer stares at a monster behind a wall or across water. By
  default it takes the nearest one, and the one with less health when two are as near. Treasure, Gem and Shard Goblins
  come first, then bosses, then magic, rare and legendary monsters. Two new boxes under Always Chase Opponent,
  Priority targets and Only reachable, switch each rule off. Update the client to use it.
- **Auto attack keeps up.** Bows, crossbows, wands and rods now grow with your level, so at high levels they stay a fair part of your damage next to spells (a crossbow hits up to 60% harder at level 2000, a wand about 70% harder from level 100). Sword, axe, club and bow hits also react more to your skill: ten more skill points now add about 70% more damage than they did at level 1000. Swords, axes and clubs were already strong and are not raised. Spells are unchanged.
- **Smoother leveling.** Levels 8 to 20 were slower than 21 to 50 (a fivefold jump at level 21): now they take about 10 minutes together. Experience no longer drops in big steps every 50 levels from 300 to 700: it fades one step at a time, so every level takes about as long as the one before. The Paragon track no longer ends at level 1282: after level 1100 you earn a point every 6 levels, until level 2192. Points you already own stay.
- **Faster late hunts.** Monsters of the late hunts (level 650 and up) have much less health, so a pack that took you 30 to 70 seconds now falls in about 10, close to the earlier hunts. They give less experience, gold and loot per kill and the kill counts of bounties, tasks and achievements went up to match: experience, gold and goals per hour stay the same, you just kill more, faster. Monster damage is unchanged. Bosses and rifts are not affected.
- **Store prices checked.** We went through every price in the store. Double Experience Potions now have a weekly limit (4 single potions and 2 packs of 5 per account, 14 hours of double experience a week), so a free player is never far behind. The Passive Tree Reset costs 100 coins (was 60) and the 13 name plate frames cost 200 (was 300). Coin packs, premium, the Chaos Pact, world buffs and the rest stay as they were. Nothing here changes your power.
- **Boss Roulette and uniques from hunts.** The Boss Roulette gave decks, a ship and mostly cards or badges. Now it
  pays crafting orbs, sockets, gold that grows with your level, now and then a unique chest of your level, badges and
  catalysts, and fewer cards. The level 900-2200 uniques now also drop from Rare and Legendary monsters in the hunts,
  not only in waystones: about one per 57 such kills, one every few hours, and a pack no longer thins their chance.
- **The other roulettes, valued by their tokens.** The Exercise, Premium and Crystal roulettes now pay crafting orbs, sockets, gold that grows with your level and now and then a unique chest, like the Boss Roulette. The Exercise one gives a chest your class can train with. The Crystal one (Tower Defense) lost its fixed gear and keeps the two best units. A spin is now worth about the hunting time its token costs.
- **Dungeons are solo too, and a test mode.** Every dungeon now takes one player: you no longer need a partner. Monsters grow with the group, +60% health and +10% damage for each extra player, so one player gets the dungeon as tuned and a group clears it faster. A queued player waits 5 seconds for a partner, then goes in. A cleared dungeon also no longer lets its monsters or boss come back while the group walks out. During the beta the server can run in test mode, which lifts weekly and daily limits (raid and guild boss locks, kill tasks, dungeon cooldown, Candlemarks and faction weekly offers). The store's limits stay.
- **Login screen services in Rust (opt-in).** The small web service behind the login screen (creature of the day, events, players online) now has a second version written in Rust, with hard limits on request size, header count and open connections, tested against the old one on the same requests. The old version stays the default; the owner can switch with one button in the admin app. You will not notice a difference: the same data shows in the same place.

## Fixes

- **Cards that did nothing now work.** Legendary cards fired only with a few stock weapons; now any sword, club, axe,
  bow, crossbow, wand or rod of the right class works, Chaos weapons and fist weapons included. The summon card no longer
  locks after the first summon. Thieves, Ninjas, Samurai, Monks, Templars and Necromancers have card lines of their own.
  Island pocket bosses now also give orbs, gear, materials and cards, not only cards.
- **Two legendary cards, more card lines.** With two legendary cards in your deck only the first could fire; now every active one can. Illusionists, Thieves, Ninjas, Samurai, Monks and Templars have a third usable common card, Mystics a fourth, and Necromancers, Liches and Bone Lords a fourth: Marrow Bolt, Soulrend Beam, Radiant Smite, Shattered Psyche, Shuriken Toss and Palm Shockwave now work with cards. A wand or rod works with either mage legendary card.
- **A real Orb of Sockets.** The Orb of Sockets no longer looks like a rune. It is now a blue glass orb with a gold
  band and three glowing sockets, in your bags, the Orbs Pouch and its tooltip. Needs the client update.
- **Big spell effects are centred.** Banner Call's golden pillar and other large effects no longer appear up and to
  the left of their target.
- **Monster and area effects centred too.** Big effects cast by monsters, area spells and critical hits now sit
  on their target as well.
- **No more black void in hunts.** The rock around every new hunt area now reaches past the edge of your screen.
- **Whole goals in the tracker.** Long goals in the Next Goals tracker now wrap to a second line instead of being cut.
- **Summons rise on fields.** A minion raised on a fire, energy or poison field puts the field out instead of dying at once.
- **Summons rise in a crowd.** Necromancer, Lich and Bone Lord minions now appear even when monsters surround you.
- **Right monsters in the teleport hall.** Some showcase cells still held old monsters, like Dragons beside a
  level 665 hunt. Every hunt now shows its own monster there, the end teleports of the top floor too. The showcase
  monsters show the level of their hunt, no two hunts show the same monster, and Wyrmspine Hollows shows an Elder
  Wyrm.
- **Potions keep up with your level.** Health and mana potions give their usual amount plus a share of your maximum
  health or mana, larger for stronger potions. The Healing of the passive tree raises both kinds.
- **Spells cost less mana.** Every spell of every class costs 40% less mana. The cheapest starting spells stay as
  they were. Damage and cooldowns did not change.
- **Orb of Fate raises the rarity.** It made most items normal. Now it lifts an item one rarity with new modifiers,
  from Normal up to Legendary. On a Legendary item it has a small chance to make it Divine, else nothing changes.
- **Helper Support skips light and rope.** Lantern, Ghost Rope and the other utility spells no longer go into the
  Support list. A list that has them loses them at the next login.
- **Readable Outfit window buttons.** The colour tabs (Head, Primary, Secondary, Detail), Copy, Paste, Ok and Cancel
  now have light letters. Update the client to see it.
- **Helper Support keeps its list after a death.** Dying no longer unticks the vigils in the list.

- **Seraphic and Dawnforged gear uses its own materials.** Their recipes asked for Myst stones and emblems, so the
  Seraphic and Dawnforged stones and emblems had no use. Each set now takes its own.
- **Myst Regalia Amulet and Ring.** Their recipes made the wrong items. They now make the amulet and the ring.
- **Tale experience fits the tale.** A tale paid a share of your own next level, so a high character got far more
  from a low tale than it was meant to give. It now pays at most what a character at the top of the tale's band gets.
- **Necromancer minions.** They are never your target, you walk through them, and they show and follow your level.
- **Store items in colour.** The Loot Bag, Orbs Pouch, Rift Key Pouch and Everfull Ration showed grey in your backpack
  and purse, as if you had locked them. They now show their colours, and they have new icons in the server's style.
  They are still store-bound. Update your client to see the new icons.
- **Gem combining works everywhere.** Using a gem stack on itself now combines it in your purse and depot too.
- **Support runes combine in your purse and depot too.** Use the stack, then left-click the same stack.
- **No stutter when the Auto Seller sells a lot.** A sale now updates your Loot Bag window once, not once per item.
- **No marks left in empty slots.** When items left a bag, the slots that became empty kept the rarity frame of the
  item that was there, and sometimes its grey or its tooltip. An empty slot is now plain, in paged windows too.
- **Empty rows of the Support spells tab.** Their boxes looked ticked but could not be clicked. An empty row's box
  is now unticked and greyed, and a spell you pick in it starts ticked.
- **Executioner's Verdict works with spells.** It finished off wounded monsters only with weapon hits. Your spells
  now execute monsters under 15% health too, and like your melee hits they deal 10% less to monsters above 60% health.
- **All gold goes to your bank.** The coins of every monster you kill now go straight to your bank account. You no
  longer need a coin on the auto loot list, and your Loot Bag no longer fills up with coins.
- **Pay from the bank everywhere.** Every shop and service that costs gold now takes it from your bank when your
  backpack has too little: NPC shops, boats and carpets, horses, outfits and addons, blessings, the crafting table, the
  Gambler, transmog, respec, gem and support removal, guild payments and the rest. Your carried coins go first, then
  the bank. The NPC trade window counts your bank too and shows the new total right after a purchase.
- **Teleports that led nowhere.** 26 teleports pointed at no tile and about 180 portals had no destination at all.
  The arena gates that draw you in work again, two portals lead back to their partner, and the dead ones are gone.
- **A way back from every hunt.** The guild hall's Teleport Hub areas have a portal back to your guild hall.
- **Every hunting room shows its monsters.** Four low-level rooms of the teleport hall stood empty; they show their
  monsters now.
- **No black void around the teleport hall.** The sea now reaches every edge you can see.
- **Our NPCs in the market, the portals at the temple.** The Hall of Services is gone. All our NPCs of Cinderhold
  now stand in the stalls of the market in the teleport room, where the old shopkeepers stood: the Oracle, the Keeper
  of Trials, the Archivist, the Warden, the Loot Buyer, the Material Broker, Osric, the Gambler, Iselde, the banker,
  the stash keeper, the house broker, the pocket boss NPC and the services clerk. Their names float over the stalls.
  The hall's portals line both sides of Cinderhold's temple entrance, between pillars on a carpet, each with its
  destination in orange above it. If you logged out in the old hall, you wake up in the temple.
- **Blazing Ember Orb is back.** Renaming the Ember Orb to Ember Shard also renamed the Blazing Ember Orb, an item of
  its own, to a name no item has. Ashseer Dravok's loot did not load, and the general crafting recipes and the shops of
  Khadin and Vazallia named an item that did not exist. They all have the Blazing Ember Orb again.
- **Fire lairs fill at once.** A few Dragon Lords, Fire Elementals and a hatchling stood on burning fire after a restart
  (they are no longer fire-immune), so they appeared only a minute later. Their spots no longer burn.
- **No Lua console.** The client's developer console (Ctrl+T) could run any script, a bot in one line. It is gone
  from players' clients.
- **Options menu names fit.** General Hotkeys, Action Bar Hotkeys and Custom Hotkeys were cut off at both edges in
  the Options window. The menu's sub-entries now use a smaller font, so every name fits, in English and Portuguese.
- **Sharp liquid orbs.** The medium and large health and mana orbs stretched art made for the small orb, so the
  ring looked blurred and blocky and the bubbles looked like square pixels. Each orb size has its own art now, and
  the bubbles are round.
- **Hand-made map changes stay.** Changes our map maker makes to the world by hand used to be lost at the next
  update. They now carry over to every update of the server.
- **Faster updates.** The client downloads eight parts of an update at once instead of four.
- **The new art downloads in parts.** The game's sprite file grew past 1 GB with this update, too big for the
  updater. It now comes as dozens of smaller files. This update downloads them all once; later art changes download
  only the few that changed.
- **New client, build 13: download it once.** The game folder now holds only the game and one locked data file. Your
  settings, hotkeys and helper presets live in your user folder (Windows: %APPDATA%\CindersOfChaos), so they start
  fresh once. Older clients can no longer log in.
- **The waystone device in your hideout works.** Using it opened nothing: the client had no window for it. Now a
  window lists the six Waystone Tiers with how many you carry, opens the portals of the tier you pick and sets your
  portal look. Using a Waystone Tier on the device, or anywhere in your own hideout, opens the same window with that
  tier picked. Without a waystone of that tier no portal opens.
- **No extra item in the stash.** Dragging an item onto the stash list sent the deposit twice, so the item to its
  right in the bag went in too, and the list did not show it. One drag now stores one item, and the server ignores a
  repeated drop from an older client.
- **Stash deposits from every page.** An item dragged from the second or a later page of your purse or depot inbox
  was refused, or the item at the same spot on the first page went in. Now the item you drag goes in.
- **Withdrawn items land in your backpack.** An item taken out of the stash could be put on in an empty equipment
  slot, even one the client does not show, or go into a stack in your Loot Bag, where the Auto Seller sold it. It now
  goes into your backpack or a bag in it, never a store pouch. When nothing fits, it stays in the stash and you are told.
- **Vigils stay off when you turn them off.** With Vigil of Clarity in the helper's Support spells tab, turning the
  vigil off made the helper cast it again at once. Now the helper unticks that row and leaves the vigil off until you
  tick it again.
- **A smaller, tidier Helper window.** The Helper window is smaller, with tighter spacing and gold headings. Options
  that did nothing on Cinders of Chaos are gone: Cure Paralyze, Auto Sell Loot, Quiver Refill, Exana Vita, the Full
  Dust alarm and the bot HUD's Cavebot and Waypoint rows. Auto Stamina and Magic Shield share one box, and Magic
  Shield shows only for a class that can cast Ward of the Mind. Your saved helper settings still load. The whole
  Helper is now in Portuguese too: every tab, window, tooltip and message.
- **Widow coins.** Nightfang Widow and Shadowcrystal Widow no longer fill your loot bag with gold, platinum and
  crystal coins. They now drop a few crystal coins at random, worth the same gold on average.
- **Mermod's tasks.** Say a task name to Mermod and the task starts. "normal <name>" and "daily <name>" work too, in
  any case, with or without the brackets of the list. Kills now count for every monster of the task, and in a party
