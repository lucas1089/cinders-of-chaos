# Fifteen spells, one ladder
Date: 2026-10-21

A class pass. Every class now has a tight kit of at most fifteen spells, attack spells wait 2, 4, 6 seconds and up from
the weakest to the strongest, and every class gets signature keystones in the passive tree. The client now updates
itself. Numbers in the balance section are calculated, not measured in game yet.

## New

- **The client updates itself.** From client build 11 on, the game checks for an update when it starts and downloads
  only the files that changed. Download build 11 once by hand; after that you only need a new download when the game
  engine itself changes, and the client tells you when.
- **Signature keystones for every class.** The Thief, Necromancer, Monk and Templar lines get two each, as strong as
  Ricochet and Deadeye:
  - Thief: Shadow Twin (your shadow repeats a hit for 60%) and Viper's Verdict (double damage on poisoned targets).
  - Necromancer: Corpse Detonation (a kill explodes) and Reaper's Toll (double damage below 40% health).
  - Monk: Seventh Palm (every fifth hit is a big one with a shockwave) and Piercing Palm (hits pass through).
  - Templar: Radiant Rebuke (shield blocks send a holy nova) and Dawnchain (melee hits chain holy damage).
  Each has a price, like the other keystones.
- **Ironhold and Wildstride.** Ironhold (Warrior line) gives more shielding, Wildstride (Ranger line) adds speed on
  top of Haste. Neither stops you from attacking.
- **Junk orbs for ember points.** The Ember Cache has an "Offer junk orbs" button: 10 low orbs give 1 ember point, up
  to 60 points a day.
- **Ember and Crown Shards become orbs.** The crafting bench's Salvage tab has two new recipes: 1,000 Ember Shards make an
  Ember Orb, 1,000 Crown Shards a Crown Orb and 1,000 Void Shards a Void Orb (Ctrl+click: up to 10 at once). The shards come from every bag in your
  backpack. They still pay for character upgrades. Looking at a shard tells you this too.
- **Level-up wings follow you.** The wings face the way your character faces, also when you turn mid-animation.

## Changes

- **Mana shows its real numbers.** Spell costs never grew with your level: the mana bar and every spell cost were
  shown multiplied by your level's display scale, so a 400 mana spell read 400,000 at level 1000. Mana, max mana and
  spell costs now show the real numbers everywhere; health and damage keep the scale. Shadow Mend costs a fixed 40
  mana instead of 2% of your mana, and no spell cost is capped by your pool any more.
- **Fifteen spells per class.** Every class and ascension keeps at most fifteen spells of its own. Spells every class
  shares (lights, Town Portal, movement, vigils, Banner Call, Soulward) do not count. 68 old spells no class used any
  more are retired. If you learned one of them, it stays in your list but can no longer be cast.
- **Cooldown ladder.** A class's attack spells now have cooldowns of 2, 4, 6 seconds and up, from the weakest to the
  strongest. A spell several classes share has one cooldown, so a few sit off their class's order.
- **Bulwark and Windrunner retired.** They stopped you from attacking for 10 seconds. Ironhold and Wildstride replace
  them.
- **Hawkeye retired.** Tautstring replaces it for the Ranger line.
- **Wands and rods scale with magic level.** Their hits used to be a flat roll. A character with a typical magic level
  hits as before; a higher magic level hits harder, up to double, a lower one softer.
- **Helper: area spells first.** Auto rotation puts area spells above single-target ones, so they are cast when enough
  monsters are around.
- **Debuffs from monsters** (slow, drunk, outfit change, lowered skills) last at most 10 seconds.
- **Reward chests** look the part and sparkle.
- **Level 500+ loot.** Gear from level 500 hunts can now roll Rare and Legendary on its own: about 4% of gear drops at
  level 500, 6% at 1000 and 8% at 2000, a third of them Legendary.
- **Scorched Marches.** The battle lock inside the zone lasts at most 60 seconds and is cleared when you leave. A defeat
  there sends you to your temple.
- **Invasions.** Invaders that got stuck or wandered off no longer keep the counter up. When the warlord falls, the rest
  flee after 90 seconds and the invasion counts as won.
- **Pocket bosses.** A boss whose corpse came out empty gives every player a personal loot roll, and stray stairs or
  portals inside the pocket are removed. The kill message says where the exit is.
- **The Void Orb corrupts.** The old Void Orb had no use; it is now the **Void Shard**. The new **Void Orb** corrupts
  a Rare or better item for good (no orb changes it afterwards, the tooltip says Corrupted), then: nothing else 28%,
  a modifier one tier up 20%, a corrupted implicit modifier 20%, 30% quality 8%, one more socket 7%, every modifier
  rolled again 8%, one rarity up (even to Divine) 4%, or a modifier two tiers down 5%. Locked and store items are
  refused. Rare drop, salvage 125 shards, Orb Exchange.
- **Depot chests** open the depot only.
- **Damage numbers** show their type (fire, ice, holy...) with the number.
- **Softer orb glow.** Orbs in your bags and the loot feed get a thin, faint teal line on the slot edge instead of the
  bright wide glow, so the orb itself is easy to see. Gear and uniques keep their glow.

## Fixes

- **Monsters kept their spells.** Retiring old spells for players took some monster attacks with them (World Devourer,
  Eradicator, Rupture, The Rage, The Hunger, Nightdweller, Phantom Shade and others). Monsters cast them again;
  players cannot.
- **The loot message lists what you really got.** On most hunts (the widows of Widowmere were the
  report) "Loot of ..." named every item the monster rolled, but part of it was taken out again right after (bigger
  packs drop less per kill), so the corpse, the Loot tab and your bag got far less than the message said. The message
  now lists exactly what is in the corpse. Nothing you looted was lost; the message counted too much. Big stacks
  (more than 100) and items a full corpse could not hold now arrive in full instead of being cut or dropped.
- **Passive tree drew owned nodes dim.** Two links were listed on one side only, so a saved path past them looked
  unallocated and the planner priced it again. Your points were never lost; the tree now shows them and never charges
  for a node you own.
- **Passive tree names.** A node that gives your class other stats now shows a name for those stats.
- **Auto build** no longer hangs on "Asking the server for a preview..." after an Apply.
- **Charges of training weapons** count down live in the tooltip.
- **Everfull potions** no longer look grey.
- **The minimap window** comes back after a death even when another window fails to restore.
- **Helper target options** survive a death.
- **Spellbook frame** no longer stays gold on several spells.
- **Capacity** shows as plain digits.
- **Text fitting** in the Auto Loot, Candlemarks, Infinity Tower and Conquest windows, and the loot splitter button.
- **Dungeon window** switches at once, and its loot shows tooltips and "Where to find".
- **Store icons, footprints and previews** are fixed; the preview shows mounts, spell effects on a troll and kill
  effects.
- **Hunt teleports** inside hunts lead back to the right portal.
- **Restart after an update.** Client build 11 could close with an error when it restarted itself after an update.
  The update was already in place, so starting the game again loaded it. Build 12 restarts cleanly. On build 11 the
  updater now asks you to close the game and start it again.
- **No older update over a newer client.** A client downloaded after an update went live could take that update's
  older files. The client now skips an update older than its own files.

## Balance

- **Class spell damage.** With the shorter cooldowns some classes hit too hard, so their spell damage is scaled: Templar
  and Sentinel x0.6; Inquisitor, Elementalist, Illusionist, Lich, Bone Lord and Shadowfist x0.7; Necromancer x0.8;
  Beastmaster x1.25. Weapon hits, wands and heals are not affected.
- **Targets kept.** Deadeye has the best single-target damage, Elementalist the best area damage.
- **Headsman's Hurl** had a five-hour cooldown in the base script; it now uses its ladder cooldown.
- **Loot by monster level.** What a corpse keeps now depends only on the monster's level, never on your level, your
  party or where you hunt. From monster level 700 on, gear that dropped as Rare, Legendary or Divine and rare drops (1%
  or less) always stay in the corpse. Coins come back as coins in every corpse that had coins, and a corpse whose other
  loot was all thinned away gets coins worth it. Gold and items per hour stay where they were. The widows of Widowmere
  used to leave an empty corpse about every second kill; now about one kill in sixteen.
