# Beta weekend 2: a gentler start, tougher rifts
Date: 2026-11-07
Kind: news

Thank you for the first weekend. Your reports shaped most of this note. It lists what changed since 31 October: a much
friendlier first hour, new ways to play together, tougher rifts and a new look for the inventory, the chat and the hotbar.

This is still a beta. Progress will be wiped before launch, so play to try things and to break them. Found a bug? Use
System > Report a Bug in the game, or tell us on Discord.

## New

- **A guided start.** New characters get a welcome path of seven first steps with small rewards, shown in Next Goals. A
  fading experience bonus helps the first levels: +50% at level 8, falling to nothing at level 150. After a death, the
  recap gives one to three tips on what to do. At level 12 a small window offers a mentor.
- **Help when you need it.** System > Help Search (Alt+F) answers a question typed in plain words. A window opens with
  a short How it works card the first time you use it. Hold Alt to read what a stat, an item line, a spell or a buff
  means. The Crafting Bench has a Guide button.
- **The Tale of Cinderhold.** A story in ten chapters from level 10 to 560, told by Archivist Maelis. Each chapter is a
  hunting mark where a named elite rises. It has achievements, a title and one Next Goal per chapter. A gold ! over an
  NPC means a quest to start, and a gold ? means one to turn in.
- **Level milestones.** Every 100 levels from 800 to 2200 gives an achievement with a title and a Milestone Cache of
  orbs, once per character. Levels 1000, 1500 and 2000 also unlock a level-up effect.
- **Group Queue.** One window for the arena, battleground and dungeon queues. A party gets a 20 second ready check
  before a match starts.
- **Weekly Challenge.** A dungeon that is the same for everyone for a week, alone or with up to four friends. Best times
  are ranked per class.
- **Weekly class ranking.** The top three of every class by weekly monster experience earn Conquest Tokens on Monday.
  Passive Tree > Build Code > Trending Builds shows what each class plays. A login streak now gives a cosmetic at 7 and
  at 60 days.
- **Build codes.** One code holds your passive tree, spells and gear. Use Copy Full Build in the Build Code panel and
  share it. The website draws it, and has a character page with gear, achievements, milestones and best runs.
- **City invasions and rumours.** Cities are attacked from time to time, and everyone who helps earns a reward, not only
  the last hit. Some invasions are two factions fighting each other. Dorrin Alewright, by the temple, sells rumours of
  where a rare monster or boss will appear in the next hour. A rumour can be false.
- **Find my item.** A button in the stash window (or Ctrl+Shift+F) searches your equipment, bags, purse, inbox and
  stash, and shows where the item is. Open backpacks are remembered per character.
- **A new inventory panel.** Tabs for Equip, Quest, Stats, Buffs and Shop, your gear, gold and Chaos Coins, and your
  backpack and every bag as pages of one grid. There is a Sort button. The classic windows stay available in the
  options.
- **Floating chat and a central hotbar.** The chat floats where you put it, and Ctrl+Shift+C hides it. The hotbar sits
  at the bottom with large cooldown numbers, two side sockets and optional health and mana globes. New characters start
  with this layout. Existing characters keep the classic one until they choose.
- **Customise Character in one window.** A large preview with a box per layer: outfit, mount, wings, aura, shader,
  outline and pet, and the colour palette next to it.
- **Better inspecting.** Inspect Player shows Paragon points, title, Bestiary rank, Boss Points, best PvP bracket and
  Collection score. Character Info has a combat sheet with attack speed, resistances and more.
- **Item cards.** The item tooltip is now a card with a rarity glow, stat tiles and requirement chips.
- **Region music.** Each place has its own looping track, from the login screen to the boss rooms, with a cross-fade.
  Options let you tune it.
- **Streamer and cinema modes.** Streamer mode (Options > Misc, or Ctrl+Shift+S) hides private messages and account
  details. Cinema mode (Ctrl+Shift+H) hides every window for clean footage. The game also takes a screenshot at big
  moments: a level up, a Legendary or Divine drop, a boss kill, an achievement or a new record.
- **Gathering feeds crafting.** Mining and herbs give by-products, 15 new refining recipes and perks at 25,
  50 and 75. Gathering nodes now stand in every hunt of the teleport room.
- **Ways out.** The raid has an exit portal after the final boss. The Infinity Tower has an exit portal and a Leave
  button.

## Changes

- **Tidier Cinderhold temple.** One of the two purple portals is gone, and every town waypoint is open for everyone.
- **Quest hall showcases.** What each showcase shows is now what its quest gives.
- **Wandering Merchant.** He is a resident of the towns now and moves to a new town every 3 hours. Prices are about two
  and a half times higher, and you can buy one of each per stop.
- **Level gates for new characters.** Some systems open later, so a new character is not buried: Season Pass 10,
  Loadouts and Charms 15, Orb Exchange and Professions 20, Transmog and Hideout 30, the Gambler and Factions 40,
  the Conquest Vendor and Guild Wars 50, Rift Atlas 100, Waystone Atlas 1100. Nothing you earned is lost.
- **Early leveling is calmer.** The experience peak is x30 instead of x45. By our model, levels 8 to 300 take about 9
  hours instead of about 7.
- **Weapon tooltips.** They name the kind of weapon, such as Two-handed sword or Fist weapon. A unique's passive stands
  out in orange. Fist weapons show their Estimated DPS.
- **Echo Shot** only rolls on distance weapons.
- **Auto setup** puts your class fight buffs, such as Poison Blades, in the Support list.
- **Hunt danger warning.** It opens in its own window at the top. The green text for using an everfull potion no longer
  covers the screen.
- **Windows an NPC opens** are no longer in the top menu: the Gambler, Candlemarks, the Conquest Vendor and Factions.
- **Windows you can move.** HUD panels and the Infinity Tower floor panel can be dragged, and the chat panel can be
  placed anywhere. Positions are saved per character.
- **Support runes** drop more from monsters of level 500 and above (x2) and 800 and above (x2.5).
- **Bosses animate** while they stand still.
- **Portuguese.** The Thief class is now called Gatuno.
- **Next server update.** These need the new server program and arrive with it: fist weapons in either hand, and an
  attack speed cap of 3 attacks per second.

## Balance

- **Rift and Omen guardians hit harder.** They have one to four extra marked attacks, and Omen guardians hit hardest.
  Rifts hold about 20% more monsters. Summons take less of a guardian's hits, and part of those hits lands on their owner.
- **Attack speed.** Class base attack intervals are lower: Shadowfist and Ninja 900 ms, Monk and Thief 1000, Iron Sage
  and Samurai 1150, all other classes 1300. Your auto attack hits for its full damage, so it deals more damage per second.
- **Class balance.** The auto rotation casts every spell on one target. Damage was retuned for the classes that ran far
  above or below the median, and spells that hit more than one target get a smaller bonus on the extra targets.
- **Ninja** takes up to 40% less damage per hit from level 650, so it can hunt its own level.
- **Physical damage.** Monsters are no longer immune to it. They take 30% less instead.

## Fixes

- A vigil aura no longer ruins the maximum health and mana of very high level characters.
- The Helper Support list is kept after a death.
- Archivist Maelis no longer fails when you ask her about the chronicle.
- Rift maps no longer put lights and props where a wall draws over them.
- The health and mana globes in the hotbar fill their ring correctly.
- A stale row in the loot filter no longer frames another item.
- A Nemesis trophy can be dropped or thrown away.
- Mirewake's temple no longer has city portals, only the waypoint.
