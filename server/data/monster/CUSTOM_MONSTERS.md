# Custom monsters

**Loading.** Lua monsters: drop a `.lua` anywhere under `data/monster/` (the server loads the whole folder, see `_template.lua.example` in `lua/chaos/`). XML monsters: put the file in `monsters/chaos/` and run `python3 -I server/tools/register_monsters.py data/monster/monsters.xml data/monster/monsters/chaos`, which adds the missing entries (safe to re-run).

**Checking.** `python3 -I server/tools/check_monsters.py items.otb 1465 data/monster` flags a lookType above the outfit count of your `Tibia.dat` (no sprite on the client), corpse or loot ids missing from `items.otb`, and, with `--known names.txt`, undefined summons.

**Importing from another server.** `server/tools/adapt_monsters.py` replaces unknown corpse ids with a fallback and drops loot lines whose item does not exist here. It does not touch lookType.

## What was imported
Our map (`exodus-spawn.xml`, `newcity-spawn.xml`) spawns 958 monster names. 98 of them (19,530 spawns) had no definition in the Exodus package, so those spawns were silent. Found definitions for 90: 85 XML from the Evolisca server (`monsters/chaos/`) and 5 Lua from Hellgrave v6 (`lua/chaos/`). Still undefined: demon (goblin), horse1, horse3, labirint, litle devil, nomadfemale, training monk, yalaharimonster (1,389 spawns; they look like placeholder names).

Adaptations (`adapt_report.txt`): 147 changes, mostly Evolisca's corpse id 41162 (not in our items) replaced by 6068, and loot lines for items that do not exist here removed. A common one is item 40407 in almost every Evolisca monster, which looks like a server-specific token: its drops are gone until we decide what it is.

**Sprites (done in the files, one step left for you).** A sprite check (`client/tools/sprites/`, report in `sprite_report.csv`) rendered each monster's lookType from a source `Tibia.dat/spr` and compared it with ours. The source pair is Black Talon's 10.98 set (`1KLKAJG2...` spr, `1xUy-ZkH...` dat in the Drive folder); Evolisca's own dat was not in the folder, and the Black Talon art matched the Evolisca names for nearly everything checked by eye, but two did not (Golden Dragon, Scaleborn: left unmapped). Result for the 98 spawned names: 37 already show the right art, 27 had the wrong art at that lookType in our dat (ids 900 to 1465 differ between servers), 24 were above our 1465 outfits, 5 have no sprite in the source, 5 have no lookType anywhere.
- 36 outfits were appended to a copy of our dat/spr as ids 1466 to 1501 and 12 monsters were pointed at an equal outfit already in our dat. `outfit_mapping.csv` lists every old to new lookType, and the monster XML files here already use the new ids.
- **You must build the merged dat/spr** (the spr is 494 MB, too big for git): `python3 -I client/tools/sprites/merge_outfits.py --src-dat <Black Talon Tibia.dat> --src-spr <Black Talon Tibia.spr> --ours-dat <Exodus Tibia.dat> --ours-spr <Exodus Tibia.spr> --report server/data/monster/sprite_report.csv --out-dir out/merged`, then use `out/merged/Tibia.dat` and `Tibia.spr` as the `data/things/1098` pair of the client. Without them the remapped monsters show wrong art.
- Verified in the sandbox: the merged dat parses (1501 outfits), the 36 new outfits render pixel-identical to the source, and 60 random original outfits are unchanged. Not verified in a real client. Six custom dat flags (39, 41, 42, 43, 46, 62) were stripped from the copied entries; their meaning is a guess.
- Still without a sprite: Golden Dragon, Scaleborn (suspect art), Mercenary, Mercenary Ranger, Mortal Illusion (`needs_sprites.txt`), and 5 names with no lookType.
