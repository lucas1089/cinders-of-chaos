Welcome to Cinders of Chaos. The world opens today, fresh for everyone. Everything from the beta has been wiped, and the
progress you make from now on is the real thing.

Found a bug? Use System > Report a Bug in the game, or tell us on Discord. Every report is read.

## New

- **Season 1, the Season of Ash.** It starts today at 15:00 and runs until 19 February 2027. Adventure > Season shows
  the Seasonal Journey, the season pass and the rules of season characters.
- **PvP Season 1.** The ranked arena and the battlegrounds start a new season today. It ends on 15 January 2027.
- **Infinity Tower, Season 1.** The tower's first season starts today.
- **Founder Pack.** A limited sale in the store until 18 December 2026 at 15:00. It never comes back. It holds
  cosmetics, a title and days of the Chaos Pact, and nothing that adds power.
- **Weekend Ritual.** Every Friday at 18:00 a themed weekend begins and lasts until Sunday 23:59. The weekend boss
  appears on Saturday at 19:00.

## Changes

- **A fresh world.** Characters, items, gold, Chaos Coins and the market from the beta are gone. Accounts are kept.
