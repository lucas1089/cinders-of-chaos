# Price sheet

Decided 2026-10-14 by the developers (owner delegated: "Decida tudo sobre o item 6"). Every number lives in a config
file; this page is the sheet and the reasons. Change a price in its config, then the places listed under "Where prices
show".

## Rules behind every price

- **Non pay-to-win, as designed.** The store sells no gear, no orb with a random result and no stat. Coins buy looks,
  convenience, time savers that the game also gives for free, and supplies at about their gold value.
- **One coin is about R$ 0,10.** Brazilian players read store prices as "10 coins per real". A 250-coin cosmetic is
  about R$ 25, a 500-coin item about R$ 50.
- **Pix first.** Most players pay by Pix (lower fees than PayPal). The USD price ends in .98, so `usdCents x brlPerUsd`
  (5) gives round Pix prices (R$ 9,90, 24,90, ...). At the 2026 exchange rate (above 5 per dollar) Pix is the cheaper
  way, which is what we want for the home audience.
- **Small top pack.** The Vault is R$ 499,90, about half the old placeholder (R$ 899,95). A big spender buys twice;
  nobody is pushed to a R$ 900 tier on a new server.

## Everfull potion tiers (O245, 2026-10-25)

Owner: "Podemos vender everfull potion na store ou em quests também." The Everfull health and mana potions have higher tiers
(great, ultimate, supreme; health, mana and spirit lines, `potions_config.lua`). Two ways to the same item, the same swap:

- **Quest (the main way):** Veyra the Apothecary swaps the flask for the next tier against 40 stock potions of that tier, from the
  level of the stock potion (80 / 130 / 200). That is 4,800 gold (great mana) to 20,000 gold (supreme health) at her single prices.
- **Store (pay to skip the hunt for the potions):** 30 / 50 / 70 coins by tier (R$ 3 / 5 / 7). The same level gate, previous
  tier and once per tier; the store gives nothing the quest does not (no better roll, no charges). `store_model.lua --check`
  keeps it at most 2x cheaper than the quest's gold at 1,000 gold a coin (it is 3.5-6.3x dearer, a convenience), a whole line
  (health 30 + 50 + 70, mana 30 + 50 + 70, spirit 20 + 30 + 50 + 70) at most 200 coins, one store offer per tier at the config's price, and no
  NPC paying for the item (it is bound: no coin -> gold loop).

- No pay-to-win power: the store sells looks, convenience and time. A boost is capped, so a free player is not far behind.

  cheaper than the gold way of the same thing; it is allowed to be dearer (a convenience).

## Skill training (O150, 2026-10-23)

Closes the open item of O140. `cd server && luajit tools/skill_model.lua` prints the tables (`--sensitivity` the assumptions,
`--check` the sanity rules); inputs and limits in `skill_model_config.lua`. Computed from the vocation XML, the engine formulas
(`Vocation::getReqSkillTries`, `getReqMana`) and `exercise_core.lua`, **not measured in game** (no engine here to run). Two inputs
are assumptions: the hunting hour (70% of it swinging, 50% casting the 8,000 mana a minute rotation) and that the base server's
stage script `skills_stages.lua` (x20 below skill 61 down to x4 from 150; magic x16 to x3) is installed as in the download.
**Since O154 (2026-10-24) the install multiplies every stage from level 101 up by 5** (`patch_skill_stages.py`, "Skill stages x5"
below); the tables here are with it. If the script is removed, every figure shrinks by the same factor for hunting and the dummy
alike, so the ratio of a dummy hour to a hunting hour does not change.

**How skills grow.** A swing or a shot is 1 try x `rateSkill` 3 x the stage; a spell is its mana x `rateMagic` 2 x the stage. A dummy
charge (every 750 ms, 4,800 an hour) is `7 x stage rate` tries (5 / 3 / 2 below 51 / 101 / 201) with no `rateSkill`, x the same
stage. Offline training (a statue, Premium, up to 12 hours a login) is half the swings of the attack speed (a quarter for the bow),
and for magic only the mana regeneration. A level needs `50 x m^(L-10)` tries (distance 30, shield 100), the magic level
`1600 x manamultiplier^L`.

### Skill stages x5 (O154, 2026-10-24)

Owner: "pode acelerar 5x o treino de skill". `tools/patch_skill_stages.py` (install step `skillstages`, config
`data/scripts/chaos/skill_stages_config.lua`: `fromLevel 101`, `factor 5`) multiplies the stage multiplier of **every skill and
the magic level** from trained level 101 up by 5 in the base server's `scripts/customs/skills/skills_stages.lua`: skills x45, x40,
x35, x20 (stock 9, 8, 7, 4), magic x35, x30, x25, x15 (stock 7, 6, 5, 3). Stages up to 100 stay (x20 to x11, magic x16 to x8).
Hunting, the exercise dummy and offline training all pass that script, so all three get x5 above 100. The tool is idempotent
(each changed row keeps `-- Chaos: was N`, a changed factor derives from those, a second run changes nothing), keeps CRLF and
refuses a file of another shape.

- **Exercise weapons.** A dummy hour is worth the same hunting hours (the factor is in both), so the coins per hunting hour

- **Still short of 140.** Hunting alone reaches 117 to 124 at level 1000, not the 140 `weapon_skill_config.lua` `typical` assumes

  hit loses `(skill/4+1)` about 7 to 14% against 140 where it lost 12 to 23%. **No damage figure was retuned** (no `--check` fails;

- **Deploy.** `install_all.py` step `skillstages` (the admin app's Update does it) and a server restart; no client change.
- **Not measured in game.** Computed from the repository and the base script's text, like the rest of O150.

## What we looked at (2026-10-14)

- Brazilian private servers usually sell "points" at about 10 per real, with a R$ 10 entry pack, by Pix.
- Large free-to-play games sell their currency in Brazil at about R$ 19-38 for the small and medium packs (one shooter:
  R$ 18,90 to R$ 379,90, bonuses growing with the pack), and their regional prices are about 3.8-4 BRL per USD, below
  the exchange rate.
- The official coins of the original game cost about US$ 12 per 250 on resale sites; a server with free premium
  features should not cost more than that.
