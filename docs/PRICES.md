# Price sheet

Decided 2026-10-14 by the developers (owner delegated: "Decida tudo sobre o item 6"). Every number lives in a config
file; this page is the sheet and the reasons. Change a price in its config, then the places listed under "Where prices
show".

## Rules behind every price

- **Non pay-to-win, as designed.** The store sells no gear, no orb with a random result and no stat. Coins buy looks,
  convenience, time savers that the game also gives for free, and supplies at about their gold value.
- **One coin is about R$ 0,10.** Brazilian players read store prices as "10 coins per real". A 250-coin cosmetic is
  about R$ 25, a 500-coin item about R$ 50.
- **Pix first.** Most players pay by Pix (lower fees than PayPal). The USD price ends in .98, so `usdCents x brlPerUsd`
  (5) gives round Pix prices (R$ 9,90, 24,90, ...). At the 2026 exchange rate (above 5 per dollar) Pix is the cheaper
  way, which is what we want for the home audience.
- **Small top pack.** The Vault is R$ 499,90, about half the old placeholder (R$ 899,95). A big spender buys twice;
  nobody is pushed to a R$ 900 tier on a new server.

Why these changes:
- **Per-character convenience** (Loot Bag, Auto Seller, Rarity Autoloot, pouches, the Ration) is bought again for every
  alt. Bag + seller + autoloot cost 1,250 coins a character (3,750, about R$ 375, for three); now 950 (2,850). That is the
  "this server is greedy" line in reviews. The Pact still lends the Auto Seller, so VIP keeps its value.
- **Classes: earned, not sold.** Selling a class reads as pay-to-win, even if the class is not stronger. Since
  2026-10-15 any character of the account at level 200 (about 3 hours of play in the leveling model of
  server/README.md "The leveling model and the hours") unlocks all three, and the store no longer sells them (was 500
  coins or level 500 from 2026-10-14). `class_unlocks_core.lua` keeps the `store` method, unused.
- **Season pass.** A season pass is usually about the price of a small pack. 600 coins with 320 back on the track is
  280 coins net (R$ 28) for a player who finishes it. The packs follow the same discount.
- **Runes stay at 1000 gold a coin.** That keeps the store rate of supplies equal to the potion packs; coins never buy
  supplies far below their gold value.

## What we looked at (2026-10-14)

- Brazilian private servers usually sell "points" at about 10 per real, with a R$ 10 entry pack, by Pix.
- Large free-to-play games sell their currency in Brazil at about R$ 19-38 for the small and medium packs (one shooter:
  R$ 18,90 to R$ 379,90, bonuses growing with the pack), and their regional prices are about 3.8-4 BRL per USD, below
  the exchange rate.
- The official coins of the original game cost about US$ 12 per 250 on resale sites; a server with free premium
  features should not cost more than that.
