# Price sheet

Decided 2026-10-14 by the developers (owner delegated: "Decida tudo sobre o item 6"). Every number lives in a config
file; this page is the sheet and the reasons. Change a price in its config, then the places listed under "Where prices
show".

## Rules behind every price

- **Non pay-to-win, as designed.** The store sells no gear, no orb with a random result and no stat. Coins buy looks,
  convenience, time savers that the game also gives for free, and supplies at about their gold value.
- **One coin is about R$ 0,10.** Brazilian players read store prices as "10 coins per real". A 250-coin cosmetic is
  about R$ 25, a 500-coin item about R$ 50.
- **Pix first.** Most players pay by Pix (lower fees than PayPal). The USD price ends in .98, so `usdCents x brlPerUsd`
  (5) gives round Pix prices (R$ 9,90, 24,90, ...). At the 2026 exchange rate (above 5 per dollar) Pix is the cheaper
  way, which is what we want for the home audience.
- **Small top pack.** The Vault is R$ 499,90, about half the old placeholder (R$ 899,95). A big spender buys twice;
  nobody is pushed to a R$ 900 tier on a new server.

Why these changes:
- **Per-character convenience** (Loot Bag, Auto Seller, Rarity Autoloot, pouches, the Ration) is bought again for every
  alt. Bag + seller + autoloot cost 1,250 coins a character (3,750, about R$ 375, for three); now 950 (2,850). That is the
  "this server is greedy" line in reviews. The Pact still lends the Auto Seller, so VIP keeps its value.
- **Classes: earned, not sold.** Selling a class reads as pay-to-win, even if the class is not stronger. Since
  2026-10-15 any character of the account at level 200 (about 3 hours of play in the leveling model of
  server/README.md "The leveling model and the hours") unlocks all three, and the store no longer sells them (was 500
  coins or level 500 from 2026-10-14). `class_unlocks_core.lua` keeps the `store` method, unused.
- **Season pass.** A season pass is usually about the price of a small pack. 600 coins with 320 back on the track is
  280 coins net (R$ 28) for a player who finishes it. The packs follow the same discount.
- **Runes stay at 1000 gold a coin.** That keeps the store rate of supplies equal to the potion packs; coins never buy
  supplies far below their gold value.

## Everfull potion tiers (O245, 2026-10-25)

Owner: "Podemos vender everfull potion na store ou em quests também." The Everfull health and mana potions have higher tiers
(great, ultimate, supreme; health, mana and spirit lines, `potions_config.lua`). Two ways to the same item, the same swap:

- **Quest (the main way):** Veyra the Apothecary swaps the flask for the next tier against 40 stock potions of that tier, from the
  level of the stock potion (80 / 130 / 200). That is 4,800 gold (great mana) to 20,000 gold (supreme health) at her single prices.
- **Store (pay to skip the hunt for the potions):** 30 / 50 / 70 coins by tier (R$ 3 / 5 / 7). The same level gate, previous
  tier and once per tier; the store gives nothing the quest does not (no better roll, no charges). `store_model.lua --check`
  keeps it at most 2x cheaper than the quest's gold at 1,000 gold a coin (it is 3.5-6.3x dearer, a convenience), a whole line
  (health 30 + 50 + 70, mana 30 + 50 + 70, spirit 20 + 30 + 50 + 70) at most 200 coins, one store offer per tier at the config's price, and no
  NPC paying for the item (it is bound: no coin -> gold loop).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| great health / spirit | 80 | 30 | 7,600 | 3.9 |
| great mana | 80 | 30 | 4,800 | 6.3 |
| ultimate health | 130 | 50 | 12,400 | 4.0 |
| ultimate mana / spirit | 130 | 50 | 14,000 | 3.6 |

O247 adds the spirit potion of tier 1 (made from the health potion of tier 1, 40 health potions at 45 gold, 20 coins: the swap that
starts the spirit line) and the supreme mana and spirit tiers (60 ultimate potions at Veyra's price, 70 coins). The spirit line is

A tier replaces the flask of its slot. Since O273 no flask has charges, so what a tier sells is the hotter roll per drink, not more drinks. A lost flask is given back by Veyra for the quest's gold (not coins).

- No pay-to-win power: the store sells looks, convenience and time. A boost is capped, so a free player is not far behind.
- One coin is R$ 0,10 and about 1,000 gold (the rate the potion and rune packs are built on). The store may be at most 2x
  cheaper than the gold way of the same thing; it is allowed to be dearer (a convenience).
- Coin packs: the same value per coin, a small bulk bonus (0 to 20%), round Pix prices.

- **Why 2 coins a slot.** An old tab of 100 entries cost 150 coins (1.5 a slot) and held a stack of 100 as one item. The
  stash merges a stack into one entry, so an entry holds more than a depot slot did. The expansion costs a little more
  per slot than an affinity tab (1.5 to 2 per entry): a specialised tab is the better deal, and it keeps the
  general tab for the odd pieces. Checked by the model: 1 to 3 coins a slot, the pack at most 15% off, all expansions at
  most 1,200 coins, an affinity tab never dearer per entry than an expansion slot.
- **One tab, not extra general tabs.** A second general tab only splits the same room. The expansion grows the one tab,
  so there is one thing to buy instead of two.
- **The old tabs are kept.** An Extra Stash Tab, an Extra Depot Tab (a pack of 4 counts as 4) or one of the three
  achievement tabs (`depot_tab_relics`, `depot_tab_trophies`, `depot_tab_vault`) is worth 4 expansions: 100 slots (owner
  2026-10-29; the tab cost 150 coins, 4 expansions are worth 200 at 50 coins). They count outside the cap of 20. A player
  with 12 bought depot tabs and 6 stash tabs gets 18 x 100 = 1,800 slots on top of the free 30.
- **Free grants that left.** The free Supplies tab, the Creature Products tab lent by the Pact and the Collectibles tab of
  the unique_50 achievement are bought now. An account that used one keeps it (one-time grant at the first login, see
  server/README.md "Stash", "Grandfathering").
- **The Guild Stash is another thing.** The guild hall chest (guild bank / stash upgrade, `guild_config.lua`) is shared
  guild storage and is not the player depot. It stays as it is.

### Pact Marks, in place of 5 coins a day

The Pact paid 5 Chaos Coins a day (150 coins, R$ 15, a month). Coins buy anything, including the Pact itself: a loop that
paid for part of the subscription. Now the Pact pays 5 **Pact Marks** a day, claimed once a day in the Pact window like the
coins were.

- **Only cosmetics.** Marks buy the six cosmetics of the shelf (`pact_config.lua` `marks.shelf`: Ember, Verdant and Azure
  loot labels 150, Cinder and Frost name colours 200, Soulspark kill effect 200). `pact.lua` accepts only those keys; no
  store offer, service, boost or supply takes Marks, and nothing turns Marks into coins. The model checks the shelf: a store
  cosmetic that is not a spell skin, a Mark worth at least a coin of the same cosmetic (at most 1.5), at least four weeks of
  Marks an item, and at most 160 Marks a month.
- **Why a shelf in the Pact window, not Marks as a second way to pay in the store.** Every store offer goes through the
  coin spend, gifts, sales, bundles, the weekly rotation and refunds. Marks in that path would touch every price rule. The
  shelf calls the cosmetic grant and nothing else.
- **What it is worth.** A month of Marks (150) buys one 150 Mark loot label. It was 150 coins of anything; it is now a
  cosmetic. That is the intent: the Pact keeps a small reward without paying for itself.
- **Per account.** Table `chaos_pact_marks` (balance, earned, spent), shown in the Pact window.

### Warnings, summary, looks

- **Expiry:** a chat line and a toast (kind `pact`, header CHAOS PACT) at login from 3 days before the end, again from the
  last day, and once online when the level changes. When the premium days end in the same window, the text names both and

- **Looks:** the name plate frame `pactbound` while the Pact is active (below a bought frame or a staff frame, above the
  default). The client has no online list, so there is no icon there. The hub spot is not built, see the open questions.

## Skill training (O150, 2026-10-23)

Closes the open item of O140. `cd server && luajit tools/skill_model.lua` prints the tables (`--sensitivity` the assumptions,
`--check` the sanity rules); inputs and limits in `skill_model_config.lua`. Computed from the vocation XML, the engine formulas
(`Vocation::getReqSkillTries`, `getReqMana`) and `exercise_core.lua`, **not measured in game** (no engine here to run). Two inputs
are assumptions: the hunting hour (70% of it swinging, 50% casting the 8,000 mana a minute rotation) and that the base server's
stage script `skills_stages.lua` (x20 below skill 61 down to x4 from 150; magic x16 to x3) is installed as in the download.
**Since O154 (2026-10-24) the install multiplies every stage from level 101 up by 5** (`patch_skill_stages.py`, "Skill stages x5"
below); the tables here are with it. If the script is removed, every figure shrinks by the same factor for hunting and the dummy
alike, so the ratio of a dummy hour to a hunting hour does not change.

**How skills grow.** A swing or a shot is 1 try x `rateSkill` 3 x the stage; a spell is its mana x `rateMagic` 2 x the stage. A dummy
charge (every 750 ms, 4,800 an hour) is `7 x stage rate` tries (5 / 3 / 2 below 51 / 101 / 201) with no `rateSkill`, x the same
stage. Offline training (a statue, Premium, up to 12 hours a login) is half the swings of the attack speed (a quarter for the bow),
and for magic only the mana regeneration. A level needs `50 x m^(L-10)` tries (distance 30, shield 100), the magic level
`1600 x manamultiplier^L`.
