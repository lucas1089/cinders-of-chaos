## 2. What data we process and why

**Website account**

- Account name, e-mail address, the country chosen at sign-up and the creation date: to create and keep the account,
  recover access and send the two-step verification codes.
- Password: we only store a code derived from it (a salted hash). Nobody, not even the staff, can see your password.
- The IP address used at sign-up: stored with the account, for security and abuse prevention.
- If you use a referral code: the code and the sign-up IP, to apply the referral program's rules.
- If you turn on "remember this device" in two-step verification: a device code, the device name and the IP, for 30
  days.

**Abuse protection on the website**

- IP addresses of failed logins: to block password guessing. Deleted after 1 day.
- IP addresses of account sign-ups and password recovery requests: to limit abuse. Deleted after 1 day.
- Password recovery links: we only store a code derived from the link. It works for 30 minutes and only once.
- Google reCAPTCHA on sign-up and password recovery, to tell people from bots (see section 5).

**In the game**

- Characters and progress: name, level, class, looks, items, gold, Chaos Coins, guild, achievements, deaths, position on
  the map and the rest of the game state. This is the service itself.
- The connection's IP address: for the limit of 2 characters per IP, bans, attack protection and technical logs.
- Technical logs of the server and the official client: error messages, refused connections and client checks (time,
  IP, account and character).
- Anti-cheat: the server measures play patterns of online characters, such as the rhythm of actions, reaction times,
  repeated routes and hours played in a row. It builds a score, may send a presence check and logs the results for the
  staff. **None of this punishes on its own:** a staff member always reviews before deciding.
- Player reports: who reported, who was reported, the reason, the note, the position, the time and the reported
  character's last 10 lines in the game's public channels. **Private messages are never stored or read.**
- Bug reports: the text you write, the position, and the character's level and class.
- Death recap: the last 5 deaths of each character, with who dealt the damage.
- Staff log: every admin action, with who did it, when and on which character.
- Game statistics: aggregate counts, such as how many characters picked each talent. These counts carry no name, account
  or identifier.

**Donations**

- Account, pack, payment method, amount, currency, status, dates, the transaction codes of Mercado Pago or PayPal and the
  generated Pix code. They are used to deliver the Chaos Coins, handle reversals and keep the records the law requires.
- For Pix, Mercado Pago requires the payer's e-mail: we send your account's e-mail address and do not keep a separate
  copy.
- **We do not receive or store** card numbers, bank account details or PayPal logins. That data stays with Mercado Pago
  and PayPal.

**Public information**

Some information is visible to everyone on the website and in the game: the characters' name, level, class, sex and
looks, the account's country flag, the last login date, deaths, guild, leaderboards and the profile comment, if you write
one. The account's other characters are shown on the profile unless you hide them. In the game, other players can see
your character's equipment and talent tree if "Allow others to inspect me" is on (the default). In the mentor program, the mentor card
only shows on the website if you choose "Show on website".

## 3. Legal bases (LGPD, art. 7)

- **Performance of a contract:** the account, the game, donations and delivering Chaos Coins.
- **Legitimate interest:** security, fraud and cheat prevention, moderation, technical logs and aggregate statistics. We
  only use what is needed, and you may object (section 8).
- **Legal obligation:** payment records and other records the law requires us to keep.
- **Regular exercise of rights:** records of bans, reports and reversals, to defend ourselves in a dispute.
- **Consent:** when you choose something optional, such as showing your mentor card on the website or remembering a
  device. You can withdraw consent at any time.

## 4. How long we keep data

| Data | Period |
|---|---|
| Account, characters, progress, sign-up IP and referral | while the account exists |
| IPs of failed logins, sign-ups and recovery requests | 1 day |
| Password recovery link | 30 minutes or until used |
| Remembered device in two-step verification | 30 days |
| Technical logs: server console, client checks, gateway servers | 14 days |
| Death recap | the last 5 deaths of each character |
| Player reports, bug reports, anti-cheat and staff logs | up to [PRAZO DOS REGISTROS DE MODERAÇÃO] |
| Bans | while the ban lasts and afterwards, as long as needed for appeals and repeat offences |
| Donation records | as long as tax and accounting laws require, at least 5 years |
| Backups | 14 days on the server and 30 days in the encrypted off-site copy |

If the law requires us to keep access logs longer, for example under the Marco Civil da Internet, we keep them for the
legal period. When a period ends, we delete or anonymize the data. A backup disappears when its own period ends.

We do not sell personal data. We only share what is needed with:

- **Mercado Pago:** processes Pix donations. It receives the amount and the account's e-mail address.
- **PayPal:** processes PayPal donations. You log in directly at PayPal.
- **Google (reCAPTCHA):** on sign-up and password recovery. Google receives browser and device data and may use its own
  cookies, under Google's privacy policy.
- **E-mail provider:** [PROVEDOR DE E-MAIL] sends the account recovery e-mails and the verification codes.
- **Hosting and attack protection:** [PROVEDOR DE HOSPEDAGEM] hosts the game, the website and the database.
  [PROVEDOR ANTI-DDOS] runs the gateway servers that filter attacks and pass connections on; they see your IP address.

- **Authorities:** when the law or a court order requires it.

## 7. Cookies

The website uses few cookies, all of them necessary:

- **Session:** keeps you logged in while the browser is open.
- **Remembered device:** only if you choose "remember this device" in two-step verification, for 30 days.
- **Language:** keeps the chosen language, when that option exists.

We use no advertising or audience analytics cookies. Google reCAPTCHA may set its own cookies on the sign-up and
password recovery pages. Videos embedded in the news come from YouTube and follow its rules.

## 8. Your rights

Under the LGPD, you may ask for:

- confirmation that we process your data, and access to it;
- correction of incomplete or wrong data;
- anonymization, blocking or deletion of data that is unnecessary or processed against the law;
- portability of your data;
- deletion of data processed with your consent, and withdrawal of that consent;
- information on who we share data with;
- review of decisions made only by automated means. In our case, no penalty is decided by a machine alone;
- objection to processing based on legitimate interest.

**How to ask:** send an e-mail to [E-MAIL DO ENCARREGADO] from the e-mail address of the account, with the account name.
If we need to confirm that the account is yours, we will ask. We answer within 15 days.

If you ask us to delete your account, we delete the account and its characters. We only keep what the law requires or
what we need to defend ourselves: donation records, bans and the moderation records until the periods of section 4.

You may also complain to the ANPD (www.gov.br/anpd).

## 9. Security

- Passwords are stored as salted hashes. The website uses HTTPS.
- Only our own servers can reach the database. The game sits behind gateway servers that filter attacks.
- Off-site backups are encrypted.
- Only authorized staff access data, and only what they need. The staff panel never shows passwords or e-mail
  addresses, and every action is logged.
- If a security incident may cause relevant risk or harm, we will tell the ANPD and the people affected, as the law
  requires.

## 10. Children and teenagers

- Anyone under 18 needs the permission of a parent or guardian to create an account and to donate.
- We do not want data of children under 12 without the specific consent of a parent or guardian. If you are a guardian
  and think a child created an account without your permission, write to [E-MAIL DO ENCARREGADO]. We will block and
  delete the account.
- We always process data of children and teenagers in their best interest, as the law requires.
