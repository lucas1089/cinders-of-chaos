# Donation Terms

Last updated: [DATA DE VIGÊNCIA]

These terms apply to donations to Cinders of Chaos, made on the website (the Donate page) or in the game (Store >
Donate). They complete the [Terms of Use](terms.en.md) and the [Privacy Policy](privacy.en.md).

## 1. A donation is voluntary

- **Donating is voluntary.** The game is fully playable for free. Nobody is required to donate.
- A donation helps keep the server, the hosting and the attack protection running.
- **A donation is not the purchase of a product.** As a thank-you, your account receives Chaos Coins. Chaos Coins are
  virtual game content and **have no real-world value**.
- Donations are received by [NOME DO RESPONSÁVEL], [CPF OU CNPJ].

## 2. Who may donate

You need a game account. If you are under 18, you may only donate with the permission of your parent or legal
guardian, using a payment method they allowed. If a minor donated without permission, the guardian may write to
[E-MAIL DE CONTATO] with the donation number.

- The Donate page and the in-game Store show each pack, its Chaos Coins and the amount **before** payment. The amount
  shown there at the time of the donation is the one that counts.

## 4. How the Chaos Coins arrive

- Once Mercado Pago or PayPal confirm the payment, the Chaos Coins reach your account within seconds, also while you
  play. The game and the website show a thank-you.
- A Pix donation that is not paid within 30 minutes expires, and nothing is charged. You can cancel a donation before
  you pay.
- Each account may have one open donation at a time and start up to 6 per hour.
- Chaos Coins stay on the account and serve all of its characters. They can only be used in the game's features, such
  as the Store. They cannot be cashed out, exchanged for money or sold outside the game.
- The Donate page shows your donation history. Mercado Pago or PayPal send the payment receipt. We do not send a
  receipt e-mail.
- If the Chaos Coins have not arrived within 24 hours of the payment confirmation, write to [E-MAIL DE CONTATO] with the
  account name and the donation number.

## 5. Refunds

- **Because a donation is voluntary, it gives no right to a refund, except where the law requires one.**
- If the law gives you a right of withdrawal or a refund, for example under the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code, we
  will honour it. To ask, write to [E-MAIL DE CONTATO] with the account name and the donation number.
- When a donation is returned, its Chaos Coins are taken back from the account, up to the balance still there.

## 6. Disputes and fraud

- If a donation is disputed at the bank, at Mercado Pago or at PayPal, or is reversed, its Chaos Coins are taken back
  from the account, up to the balance still there.
- Disputing a donation you made yourself, to keep the Chaos Coins for free, is fraud. So is using someone else's
  payment method without permission. In those cases the Chaos Coins are taken back as above, we may remove what was
  obtained with them, and the account is locked until the dispute is settled. Fraud again means a permanent ban,
  decided by an administrator. The penalties are on the [Rules page](/page.php?p=rules), section 7.
- A dispute made in good faith is not fraud, for example Chaos Coins that never arrived or a payment charged twice.
  Please write to [E-MAIL DE CONTATO] first; we answer before you need the bank.

## 7. Season Pass, creator codes and the Character Bazaar

- **Season Pass.** The premium track of a season pass is unlocked with Chaos Coins (600 Chaos
  Coins). It gives cosmetic items and some Chaos Coins back only, never gold, experience or game power. Its rewards
  are those of the season it belongs to, and can be claimed until 14 days after that season
  ends. Buying it late gives the rewards of the tiers already reached at once.
- **Creator codes.** A creator code chosen on the website does not change your donation or the Chaos Coins you get. As
  a thank-you from the server, the creator receives 10 % of the Chaos Coins of your donations made
  while the code is chosen, in the game, after 14 days. Nothing of this is paid in money.
  A creator code cannot be used by its own account, nor by accounts of the same person, connection or payer. A refunded
  or disputed donation gives the creator nothing. The creator agrees to these rules and to tell its audience that the
  code supports it ([REGRAS DE DIVULGAÇÃO DO CRIADOR]). We may switch a code off at any time.
- **Character Bazaar.** Players may sell a character to another player on the website for Chaos Coins only. The
  server keeps a fee of 10 % of the final price. The bid is held from the bidder's Chaos Coins until the
  auction ends and given back at once if the bidder is outbid or the auction is cancelled. At the end, the character
  moves to the winner's account with what it carries, and the seller receives the price less the fee. Season,
  Hardcore and Self-Found characters, guild leaders, characters with an open report and characters of an account that
  is banned or has a disputed donation cannot be sold. Staff may cancel an auction at any time, for example on a report
  of fraud; every bid then goes back. A character bought on the Bazaar is not a purchase from us and has no
  real-world value; the Chaos Coins paid are not returned in money. Selling or buying characters, items or Chaos
  Coins for money or anything outside the game remains forbidden (Rules page, section 7).

## 8. Bans, wipes, seasons and changes

- An account banned for breaking the rules has no right to a refund of its donations. During a temporary ban, the
  Chaos Coins stay on the account for when it ends.
- Chaos Coins and what they bring in the game can change over time: offers, coin prices, seasons and balance. Season
  items follow the season's rules.
- A full wipe after launch only happens after a notice of at least [PRAZO DE AVISO DE WIPE] days, as the
  [Terms of Use](terms.en.md) say.
- If the game closes for good, we will give notice first, as the Terms of Use say. Donations are not returned, except
  where the law requires.
