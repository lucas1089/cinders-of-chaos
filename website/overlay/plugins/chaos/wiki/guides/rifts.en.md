# Rifts and the Atlas

Rifts are the main endgame content. Every rift is a new map, generated on the spot, with a time limit and a guardian at the end.

## The key

- A [Rift Key](wiki:rift:keys) opens a rift for you and your party, up to {{rift.maxPlayers}} players.
- A key has a tier from 1 to {{rift.maxTier}} and up to {{rift.maxMods}} modifiers. The [modifiers](wiki:tab:rift) make the rift harder and the rewards bigger.
- Keys drop from monsters, from finished rifts and from the rift chest. Orbs change keys too.

## The run

=> @key | Rift Key | Tier 1 to {{rift.maxTier}}, up to {{rift.maxMods}} modifiers.
=> @rift | Open the rift | You and your party, up to {{rift.maxPlayers}} players.
=> @riftclear | Clear the map | At {{rift.bossAt}}% of the monsters, the guardian comes.
=> @riftguardian | The guardian | Kill it before the time runs out.
=> @chest | Your chest | Gold, orbs, items, gems and supports.
=> @atlas | Atlas points | First completions give Atlas points.

- Kill the map's monsters. Once {{rift.bossAt}}% of them are dead, the guardian comes.
- The party shares a few lives. If time runs out, the rift fails and the key is spent.
- When you win, each player opens their own chest: gold, orbs, items, gems and supports. See [tiers and rewards](wiki:rift:tiers).

> [warn] If the time runs out, the rift fails and the key is spent.

## Omens

Rifts of tier {{rift.omenMinTier}} or more give fragments. {{rift.omenKeyCost}} fragments make an Omen Key, which opens an Omen guardian's arena. See [Omens](wiki:rift:omens). Above them are the [Ascended Omens](wiki:boss:ascended).

## The Atlas

The [Rift Atlas](wiki:rift:atlas) is a map of bonuses for your rifts. Points come only from first completions: each theme and tier sealed, each tier without a death, each tier within its Swift time and each Omen guardian. That is {{atlas.points}} points in all. Spend them on small nodes, notables and one keystone.
