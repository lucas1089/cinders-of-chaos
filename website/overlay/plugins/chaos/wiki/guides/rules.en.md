# Rules and reporting

The rules keep the game fair. By playing you accept the [rules](/page.php?p=rules) and the [Terms of Use](legal:terms).

## The essentials

- No bots, macros or modified clients. The game's own tools are allowed: hunt tracker, loot filter, autoloot and auto seller.
- The official client's helper (healing, spell rules, auto rotation) is allowed while you play. Leaving it running for hours with nobody at the keyboard counts as botting: a warning the first time, then the botting penalties.
- Do not use bugs. Report them with System > Report a Bug. A bug you report yourself is never punished.
- Be respectful in every channel. No harassment, threats, hate or spam.
- No advertising of other games, servers or websites.
- Never ask for or share passwords. Staff will never ask for yours.
- No selling accounts, characters, items or gold for real money outside the official store.

## How to report a player

Right-click the player and pick Report Player, or use System > Report a Player. Choose the reason and write a short note. You need level {{reports.minLevel}} or more, and each account can send up to {{reports.perDay}} reports a day. Staff read every case. The system never bans anyone on its own.

## Penalties and appeals

Penalties go from a warning to a permanent ban, by how serious the offence is and how often it happens. If you disagree with a decision, appeal calmly in the appeals channel of our Discord. An administrator who did not give the penalty reads it.
