# Picking a class

There are {{class.lines}} class lines. Each starts with a base class and ascends into one of two classes. That is {{class.count}} classes in all and {{spell.count}} spells.

## The free classes

- **[Warrior](wiki:class:warrior)**: melee, with a shield or a two-handed weapon. Ascends into the Juggernaut (tank) or the Berserker (area damage).
- **[Ranger](wiki:class:ranger)**: bows and crossbows from range. Ascends into the Deadeye (single target) or the Beastmaster (with companions).
- **[Mystic](wiki:class:mystic)**: elemental spells. Ascends into the Elementalist (damage) or the Illusionist (healing and support).
- **[Thief](wiki:class:thief)**: quick blades and poison. Ascends into the Ninja or the Samurai.

## The unlockable classes

{{class.lockedNames}} unlock for your whole account once any of its characters reaches level {{class.unlockLevel}}, by playing. They are never sold.

- **[Monk](wiki:class:monk)**: bare-handed fighting with chi. Shadowfist or Iron Sage.
- **[Necromancer](wiki:class:necromancer)**: the undead and curses. Lich or Bone Lord.
- **[Templar](wiki:class:templar)**: sword, shield and holy light. Inquisitor or Sentinel.

## Ascension

At level {{class.ascensionLevel}}, talk to the Ascendant Oracle in the market of Cinderhold's teleport room (the two great teleports north of the temple lead there). First comes the Proof of Strength: {{ascension.proofPoints}} points from rare and legendary monsters. Then the Trial: you alone against a guardian, within {{ascension.trialTime}}.

=> @level | Level {{class.ascensionLevel}} | Talk to the Ascendant Oracle in the market of the teleport room.
=> @drop | Proof of Strength | {{ascension.proofPoints}} points from rare and legendary monsters.
=> @guardian | The Trial | Alone against a guardian, within {{ascension.trialTime}}.
=> @ascend | Ascended | Your new class, with its own spells.

> [warn] If you fail the Trial, you can try again after {{ascension.retry}}.

## Which one?

There is no wrong choice. For a first character, the Warrior and the Ranger are the simplest. If you like groups, the Illusionist or the Sentinel do well. Each class page lists the line's spells, and the [passive tree](wiki:guide:tree) guide has example builds.
