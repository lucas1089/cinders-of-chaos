# PvP rules

The world is open to PvP, with safe zones and ranked modes where nobody really loses anything.

## In the open world

- Outside protection zones, players can attack other players. Temples and depots are protection zones.
- Unjustified kills give a skull. Many of them lead to heavier skulls, with bigger losses on death.
- You may have up to {{multiclient.perIp}} characters online per connection. The second one cannot attack players or enter dungeons.

## Arena

::: if arena.on
Ranked PvP in 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3, with a rating per mode. From level {{arena.minLevel}}, queueing with Adventure > Arena from a protection zone. Nobody really dies in the arena and there are no skulls.
::: end

## Battleground

::: if bg.on
Ember Wells is an objective battleground, from 5v5 to 10v10, from level {{bg.minLevel}}. Open Adventure > Battleground. Wins give honor to spend at the honor vendor.
::: end

## The Scorched Marches

::: if pvpzone.on
{{pvpzone.name}} is an open PvP zone with risk and reward, from level {{pvpzone.minLevel}}. You enter with Adventure > Scorched Marches, from a protection zone. A defeated player drops the embers they carry, and the winner can take part of them. Hardcore season characters cannot enter.
::: end

## Guilds

Guild wars and the Guild Siege have rules of their own. See [Social](wiki:guide:social) and [Raids, bosses and events](wiki:guide:bosses).

## Not allowed

- Blocking spawns, quest rooms or boss portals on purpose for long periods.
- Manipulating the leaderboards, the arena or the siege: trading wins, feeding kills, boosting with extra characters.

Read the [full rules](/page.php?p=rules).
