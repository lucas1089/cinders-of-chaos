# The passive tree and example builds

The passive tree has {{tree.nodes}} nodes. You will never have points for all of them, so every choice counts.

## Points

- One point every {{tree.levelsPerPoint}} levels above level {{tree.startLevel}}. That is about {{tree.points300}} points at level 300 and {{tree.points1000}} at level 1000.
- Some bosses give extra points.
- You can keep up to {{tree.presets}} presets. A full reset costs {{tree.resetPerLevel}} gold per level. A single node can be refunded too.
- After level {{paragon.startLevel}} you get Paragon points, for small capped bonuses.

## Kinds of node

- **Small nodes**: a little of a stat.
- **[Notables](wiki:tab:tree)**: strong named nodes along the paths. There are {{tree.notables}}.
- **Keystones**: change how you play, always with a price. There are {{tree.keystones}}. Example: [Hollow Vessel](wiki:tree:96) sets your health to 100 and trades it all for a large energy shield.
- **Masteries**: you pick one effect and can change it later.

## Example builds

Each example works with the base class and shines with one ascension.

- **Pyre Walker** (Juggernaut): [Pyre Oath](wiki:tree:108). Walk into the pack and stand there; the fire aura does the killing. Very tough, weak on one target.
- **Stormquiver** (Deadeye): Stormstring. Every arrow chains lightning through the pack. Clears from range.
- **Rime Orb** (Elementalist): Winter's Lattice. Freeze the pack; every kill shatters into the next.
- **Trapper** (Ranger or Deadeye): [Snarewright's Creed](wiki:tree:105) with [Snare Trap](wiki:spell:99). Throw traps into the pack's path and keep shooting.
- **Choir Herald** (Sentinel, Illusionist or Bone Lord): [Covenant of Vigils](wiki:tree:99) and three Vigils on for the party.
- **Hollow Caster** (Elementalist or Lich): Hollow Vessel and energy shield modifiers. Immune to poison.
- **Thorns** (Sentinel): the [Thorns](wiki:tree:624:sentinel) keystone hits back whoever hits you. It is about 25 points from the start, so it suits level 300 or more.

## Tips

- Keystones need the path to them. Plan before you spend.
- The client's planner shows the cost in points and lets you save and share the build code.
- Every keystone and notable is in the [tree reference](wiki:tab:tree).
