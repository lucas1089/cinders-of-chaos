# FAQ

Short answers to the most common questions.

## Is the game free?

Yes. Everything can be played for free. The store is almost all cosmetic and donations are voluntary. See [The store and donations](wiki:guide:store).

## How do I start?

Create the account and the character on the website, download the client and log in. Read [Getting started](wiki:guide:start).

## Which class should I pick?

To start, the Warrior or the Ranger are the simplest. See [Picking a class](wiki:guide:classes).

## I made a mistake in the passive tree. Now what?

You can refund a node or reset the whole tree for gold, and keep presets. See [the passive tree](wiki:guide:tree).

## Where do I see where an item drops?

In game, the Codex and the Item Finder show the sources. Here, see the [uniques reference](wiki:tab:uniques).

## Can I play two characters at once?

Up to {{multiclient.perIp}} per connection. The second one cannot attack players, cannot enter dungeons and gets no rewards from bosses, bounties or leaderboards.

## When does the season start?

The {{season.name}} runs from {{season.start}} to {{season.finish}}. See [Seasons and leaderboards](wiki:guide:seasons).

## I found a bug. What do I do?

Use System > Report a Bug in game. Reporting a bug is never punished.

## How do I reach the staff?

On our Discord. Staff never ask for your password.
