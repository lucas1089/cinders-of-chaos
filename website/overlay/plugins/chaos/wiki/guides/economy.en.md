# The economy

Gold, orbs and Chaos Coins move between players. This guide shows where to earn and where to trade.

## Gold and the bank

Gold comes from monsters, bounties, rifts and events. Keep it in the bank: the market, the Orb Exchange and many fees use your bank balance.

## Orbs are the currency

To many players the crafting orbs are worth more than gold. Use them on your items or trade them.

## The market

- Economy > Market, or the market item in your depot. From level {{market.minLevel}}.
- Sell for gold, for Chaos Coins or for both. Offers last {{market.durations}} days.
- A listing fee of {{market.listingFee}}% and a sales tax of {{market.tax}}%. They take gold out of the game and hold inflation back.
- Equipped items, locked items and store items cannot be listed.

## The Orb Exchange

Economy > Orb Exchange is an order book for orbs: orb for gold or orb for orb. From level {{orbx.minLevel}}. A fee of {{orbx.goldFee}}% on the gold of every trade. Orders last {{orbx.durations}} days.

## Other sources

- Adventure > Bounty Board has daily contracts that pay gold and orbs.
- The [professions](wiki:mat:professions) give materials to sell to the Material Broker.
- Salvage breaks items down into useful shards.
- The daily reward follows a week of {{daily.days}} days.

## Runes

No spell makes runes. Buy them from the rune sellers (Raloth in Mirewake and Isora in Cinderhold sell every rune, other magic shops sell some), or craft them at the general crafting tables: unscribed runes from the sellers, herbs and ore pieces you gather, and some gold. A crafted rune costs about half the shop price.

## Take care

Trading for real money outside the official store is not allowed. See the [rules](wiki:guide:rules).
