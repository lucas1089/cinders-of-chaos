# Progression: what to do next

Lost? In the game, open **Adventure > Next Goals**. It lists the goals of your level band: what to do, why it matters, where to go (one click opens the window or marks the place on your map), your progress, a small reward and how long it takes. A tracker on screen shows the next three. The game has {{goals.bands}} bands and {{goals.count}} goals, from level 1 to {{goals.lastBand}} and beyond. This guide is the same path on one page: where your next gear comes from, band by band.

=> @level | Next Goals | Adventure > Next Goals: goals, gear path and hunts of your band.
=> @reward | Tracker | The next three goals on screen; click one to open the panel.
=> @temple | Show on map | A goal's button marks the NPC, the hunt or the teleport on your minimap.
=> @drop | Item Finder | Economy > Item Finder: where any item drops, who sells it.

> [tip] Hover any item: its tooltip names where it drops and, for rare items, about how many kills it takes.

Gifts wait for you at levels {{newplayer.giftLevels}}: potions, orbs and, later, Orbs of Fate and Grafting.

## Level 1-19: First steps

- Your starter kit carries you to level 20. Town shops sell weapons and armour for gold; the Loot Buyer in the Hall of Services (the portal beside the temple) buys your loot.
- Magic (blue) monsters drop gear with a rarity: wear what is better.
- Goals: reach level 8, slay a magic monster, finish a bounty contract, spend your first passive point.

## Level 20-49: Finding your feet

- Rare (yellow) monsters drop better rolls. Orbs of Upheaval reroll a piece on the Crafting Bench (Alt+C).
- Gems socket into gear; the Item Finder shows where each one drops.
- Goals: a bestiary kill task, your first crafting orb, 50 tiered monsters, a city invasion.

## Level 50-99: Adept

- Bounty contracts pay gold and orbs every day. The Gambler sells sealed pieces of your level.
- Legendary (orange) monsters can drop uniques.
- Goals: win an arena match, 10 bounties, 10 rare monsters, 25 creatures in the Bestiary.

## Level 100-199: Ascension

- Ascend at the Ascendant Oracle, then open rifts, take the Vocation Trial and the {{descent.name}}.
- Rift chests roll a unique at the rift's loot tier. Dungeon boss chests (Alt+J) rise in item level with the difficulty.
- Faction quartermasters sell fixed gear at Honored and Revered.
::: if pocket.on
- From level 150, pocket bosses: a private copy of a boss from Adventure > Boss Spawns, {{pocket.charges}} charges a day.
::: end

## Level 200-299: Veteran

- The hunt teleports of Cinderhold open: Bonechoir Catacomb and Sulphur Gallery.
- Conquest Tokens from every boss and clear buy any unique you have seen, at a fixed price (Iselde Varrow).
- Archivist Maelis pays tales with gear and orbs.

## Level 300-499: World bosses

- World bosses come every 6 hours from level {{worldboss.minLevel}}; each rolls a piece of its set and a boss unique for every helper. The first kill of each major boss gives a passive point.
- Pocket bosses give the same bosses in a private copy, with less loot.
- Orbs of Fate improve one modifier; the Cruel and Merciless Trials open their node pairs.

## Level 500-799: Hardened

- New hunts: Widowmere, Wyrmspine Hollows and Pyrarch Crucible, then Hexwood Thicket and Corsairs' Reach.
- The teleport hall of Cinderhold has a hunt every 15 levels or so: floor 6 for levels 500-1000, floor 5 for 1000 and up.
- Faction gear at Revered; Orbs of Grafting add a modifier. Higher rift tiers drop legendary loot.

## Level 800-899: Omens and the raid

- The Ashen Sepulchre raid (level {{raid.minLevel}}) drops the raid uniques.
- Omen guardian chests roll several uniques at the boss tier.
- The Hollow Vault, the first late dungeon, has its own boss chest.

## Level 900-999: Ascended Omens

- The Ascended Omens alone drop the Ascended uniques (and the Cinderheart later).
- Hunt Mirefang Basin before level 1000.

## Level 1000-1299: The Cinderheart

- The Cinderheart and The Marrow Crypts. Past level 1000 every level is a Paragon point.
- Hunts: Rimecrown Depths, Slagmourn Foundry, Cinderglass Sanctum, Hollowmass Reliquary.
- Legendary powers drop on legendary items; the Codex lists every power.

## Level 1300-1599: The deep

- The Iron Crucible and its boss chest.
- Hunts: Gallowfen Mire, Voidscar Chasm, Gravehorn Deeps.

## Level 1600-1999: The abyss

- The Fallen Sanctum and the hardest Sepulchre runs.
- Hunt Duskmarrow Crater from level 1750.

## Level 2000+: The apex

- The Pyre Throne, the last dungeon; its best chest is at difficulty {{dungeon.difficulties}}.
- Conquest Tokens buy the uniques luck has not given you.

## Luck and effort

Every drop chance in the game is public: the Item Finder lists it per monster, the [Uniques](wiki:tab:uniques) pages show the expected effort ("~1 in 500 legendary kills") and the [Monsters](wiki:tab:monsters) pages show each loot table. When luck does not come, the Conquest vendor sells what you have seen at a fixed price, and bad-luck protection raises your chance after every miss.
