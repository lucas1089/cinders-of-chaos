# Seasons and leaderboards

Seasons give everyone a fair fresh start. The leaderboards show who went furthest each week.

## Seasons

A season is a fresh start, with new characters and a ladder of their own. The first one is the **{{season.name}}**, from {{season.start}} to {{season.finish}}. Season bonus: **{{season.modifierName}}**. {{season.modifier}}

=> @create | New character | Up to level {{season.joinMaxLevel}}, an empty depot, no party.
=> @season | Join | Adventure > Season, then pick a mode.
=> @ladder | Play | The Seasonal Journey and the season track.
=> @reward | Keep the rewards | The season track's rewards stay with the account.

- To join, open Adventure > Season with a new character, up to level {{season.joinMaxLevel}}, with an empty depot and no party.
- Season characters receive nothing from standard characters.
- When you join you pick a mode: Standard, Self-Found (only what you find yourself), Hardcore (one death ends the run) or Hardcore Self-Found. The mode stays until the season ends.
- The Seasonal Journey walks the character through the content, chapter by chapter.
- The rewards of the season track belong to the account and stay with you afterwards.

> [note] Hardcore and Hardcore Self-Found: one death ends the run.

## Season Pass

The Season Pass is for the whole account, with {{seasonpass.tiers}} tiers on a free track and a premium track. Pass experience comes only from playing: daily and weekly challenges, hunting and content. Open it with Adventure > Season Pass.

## Leaderboards

- There are {{boards.count}} weekly boards: world bosses, rifts, bounties, arena, siege, dungeons and more. They reset every Monday at {{boards.reset}} (server time).
- The top players get a title for a week, and many boards pay orbs.
- The Class Ladder shows the highest level of each class, for all time.
- See them in game with Social > Leaderboards, or on the website's [leaderboards](page:leaderboards) page.
