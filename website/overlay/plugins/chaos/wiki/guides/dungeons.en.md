# Dungeons, the Tower and the Descent

Instanced content to play alone or in a group.

## Dungeons

- There are {{dungeon.count}} [dungeons](wiki:dungeon:about) with {{dungeon.difficulties}} difficulties each.
- Step on the entry or open Adventure > Dungeons, pick a difficulty and join the queue. The group goes in together.
- Kill the monsters, then the boss. Each difficulty you finish unlocks the next.
- Higher difficulties give more experience, gold and loot.
- Speed and flawless challenges give points and count for the rankings.

## The Infinity Tower

The [Infinity Tower](wiki:dungeon:tower) is a climb from floor to floor, with waves and boss floors. Each floor is worth {{tower.pointsPerFloor}} points. Tower seasons last {{tower.seasonDays}} days, with milestone rewards and chests for the top three.

## The Ember Descent

::: if descent.on
[{{descent.name}}](wiki:dungeon:descent) is a roguelike run: generated rooms, a boon to pick after each room and a boss at the end. For 1 to {{descent.maxPlayers}} players from level {{descent.minLevel}}. There is one free run a day and a weekly run with its own leaderboard.
::: end
