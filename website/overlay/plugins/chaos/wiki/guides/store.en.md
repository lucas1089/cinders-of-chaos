# The store and donations

The game is fully playable for free. Nobody is required to donate.

## Chaos Coins

- The store is in the game, behind the Store button. Prices are in Chaos Coins, which belong to the account.
- Chaos Coins are a thank-you for a **voluntary donation** made on the website, on the [Donate](page:coins) page, with Pix or PayPal.
- A donation is not the purchase of a product, and Chaos Coins have no real-world value. Read the [Donation Terms](legal:donations) before you donate.
- Chaos Coins can also be traded for gold on the market, between players.

## What the store has

The store has {{store.offers}} offers in {{store.categories}} categories. Most of them, {{store.cosmetic}}, are cosmetic: outfits, mounts, wings, auras, effects and decoration. The rest are account services and conveniences, such as extra storage and depot tabs, plus boosts, consumables, pets, the Season Pass and the unlock of the {{class.lockedNames}} classes.

The full list, with the type of every offer, is in the [store reference](wiki:store:about).

## Good to know

- Staff never ask for your password and never give items, gold or coins.
- Disputing your own donation to keep the coins is fraud. See the [rules](/page.php?p=rules).
