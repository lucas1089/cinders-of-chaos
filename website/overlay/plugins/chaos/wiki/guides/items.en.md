# Items and crafting

Items are the heart of the game. One good drop changes your build.

## Rarity and modifiers

- There are {{item.rarities}} rarities, from {{item.rarityFirst}} to {{item.rarityLast}}. The rarity decides how many modifiers an item has.
- Every item has an item level, from 1 to {{item.maxItemLevel}}. It decides the tiers of its modifiers, from T1 to T{{item.tiers}}.
- Prefixes are damage and defence. Suffixes are utility, resistances and speed.
- From {{item.powersFrom}} up, an item can roll a legendary power.
- See [how items work](wiki:basics:items) and the list of [modifiers](wiki:tab:modifiers).

## Uniques and sets

There are {{item.uniques}} [uniques](wiki:tab:uniques), with fixed stats that no orb changes. There are also {{item.sets}} [sets](wiki:tab:sets): wearing several pieces of one set gives extra bonuses.

## Orbs

[Orbs](wiki:tab:orbs) change items. Use the orb on the item, or open Economy > Crafting Bench to see the result first. A few examples:

- Orb of Awakening and Orb of Gilding give a plain item modifiers.
- Orb of Grafting adds a modifier. Orb of Unmaking removes one.
- Orb of Upheaval rerolls the modifiers. Orb of Fate rolls a new rarity, often none.

=> @drop | Find an item | Monsters, rifts and bosses drop gear.
=> @bench | Crafting Bench | Economy > Crafting Bench shows the result first.
=> @orb | Use an orb | Add, reroll or remove modifiers.
=> @gem | Gems | Socket gems for more stats.
=> @salvage | Salvage | Gear you do not need becomes rune shards, and shards become orbs.

> [tip] Uniques have fixed stats: no orb changes them. Craft on items with a rarity.

## Gems and supports

Gear can have up to {{gem.maxSockets}} [sockets](wiki:gem:sockets) for [gems](wiki:tab:gems). Your spells take [supports](wiki:tab:supports), up to {{support.sockets}} per spell, that change how they work.

## Professions

Four [gathering professions](wiki:mat:professions) give materials to sell to the Material Broker next to every temple. Levelling them also gives a little extra experience.
