# Getting started

Cinders of Chaos is a dark fantasy online RPG with its own client. You hunt, collect loot and build your character your way. This guide covers the first minutes.

## Account, client and character

- Create an account on the website and accept the Terms of Use.
- Download the client from the downloads page and log in with your account.
- Create a character on the website. The free classes are {{class.openNames}}.

## The tutorial island

At your first login you arrive on a small island. Each station teaches one thing: fighting, spells, the passive tree and orbs. You get a passive point and an Orb of Upheaval to practise with. Nobody dies on the island. When you leave, you go to the temple of Cinderhold with your class kit.

> [tip] Already know the game? Skip the tutorial at the goblin statue.

## Cinderhold, the main city

Every new character lives in Cinderhold, the biggest city. Everything is close to its temple:

- Two great teleports, five steps north of the temple, lead to the teleport room: a portal to a hunt of every level.
- The stash, two banks and the shops.
- The market of the teleport room (the two great teleports north of the temple lead there): every service of the game in its stall. The Loot Buyer, who buys what you find, the Material Broker, the Gambler, the Conquest and Candlemark vendors, the Ascendant Oracle and the Keeper of Trials.
- The portal gallery along the temple's south wall: the portals to your hideout, the guild hall, the Ember Descent, the raid, the Memorial, the Ashen Chronicle and the events, each with its destination in orange letters over it.
- Death, the Town Portal spell and the temple scroll all bring you back to this temple.

## The first levels

- Open Character > Spellbook to see your spells. The Town Portal spell takes you back to town.
- Spend your points in the [passive tree](wiki:guide:tree). A point comes every {{tree.levelsPerPoint}} levels.
- Take contracts from Adventure > Bounty Board. They pay gold and orbs.
- Claim the daily reward. A streak of days pays more.
- At levels {{newplayer.giftLevels}} you get gifts: potions, orbs and more.
- Lost later on? Adventure > Next Goals shows what to do at your level. See [Progression](wiki:guide:progression).

## Hunting in packs

Monsters come in packs and die fast. Pull a group and kill it together with your area spells: several kills within 3 seconds of each other form a chain, and a chain of 4 or more gives up to 40% more experience. Each kill gives less experience and loot than one big monster would, so a full pack is the fast way up. Bosses keep their full strength. See [Pack combat](wiki:basics:pace).

## The top menu

Almost everything is in the client's top menu: Character, Adventure, Economy, Social and System. You do not need to learn commands.
